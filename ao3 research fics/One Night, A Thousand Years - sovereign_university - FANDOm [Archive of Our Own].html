One Night, A Thousand Years

sovereign_university

Summary:

Vi and Caitlyn quietly celebrate their anniversary with small but meaningful gifts and a shared moment of trust and love. The night is filled with tenderness, memories, and a soft reconnection that shows how strong their bond truly is.

Notes:

This felt natural to write since parts of it reflect my own experiences with love and waiting. Sometimes it’s the little things, the quiet moments, that mean the most. Thanks for reading and I hope you enjoy all the feels!

Work Text:

♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡

Vi’s footsteps fell softly against the cracked pavement as she moved toward the restaurant, the cool night air wrapping around her like a whispered secret. The package hidden beneath her leather jacket pressed firmly against her side, its weight a steady reminder of tonight’s importance. She forced herself to breathe evenly but her mind raced, tangled in half-formed hopes and fears.

Will she understand what this means to me? Can tonight break the years of quiet between us?

These thoughts tangled with memories, bursting laughter, silent fights.. tender promises and the ache of absence that never quite faded. She pictured Caitlyn’s eyes catching the candlelight just right their hands finding each other like they always had words becoming unnecessary in the space between them.

Weeks ago Vi had made the reservation quietly telling the restaurant it was their anniversary. She had asked for a small quiet table near a window softened by the flicker of candlelight. It was a subtle plan to let the night acknowledge their bond to ease the weight of everything left unsaid.

The warm light spilled out onto the sidewalk as Vi stepped inside, the murmur of voices and soft clinking of glasses wrapping around her. Her eyes immediately found Caitlyn seated by the window serene and strong beneath the gentle glow. Caitlyn wore a violet dress that clung like twilight itself, the fabric flowing just past her knees. The sleeveless cut revealed toned arms the sleeves casually rolled to her elbows. Her bare unmasked eye caught the candlelight flashing with life and the faint scar that spoke of battles neither of them forgot.

Vi slid into the chair opposite Caitlyn, heart thudding hard enough she thought it might be heard in the quiet. Her hands trembled slightly as they found Caitlyn’s holding tight for comfort.

The waitress approached with a warm smile the soft pop of the champagne bottle filling the space around them. She poured the pale bubbles into the glasses with practiced grace rising gently sparkling as the light caught them. She set the flutes down carefully her eyes shining kind and curious.

“Happy anniversary,” she said softly. Her gaze flicked between them gentle but direct. “We were told it’s a special night. So what’s your secret?”

Vi blinked startled by the simple but heavy question. She looked to Caitlyn and felt calm wash over her in the steady warmth of that gaze.

“Sacrifice always comes first,” Vi said voice low but sure each word weighted with hard-earned truth.

Caitlyn’s hand squeezed hers gently as she replied in a quiet voice “Trust is found in moments beyond words.”

A soft smile curved Vi’s lips. The tension that had clawed at her chest slipped away on the champagne’s delicate fizz. Time stretched thin and fragile a thread weaving their past and present.

Beneath her jacket Vi slid out the carefully wrapped package and pushed it across the table hope shining in her eyes. Caitlyn’s surprise bloomed wide and then melted into tenderness. Her fingers slowly undone the crimson ribbon and peeled back the paper with reverence. Inside lay a golden locket smooth and glowing softly like the memories it held.

“I wanted you to have something,” Vi whispered voice trembling with all the quiet love she held inside “to remind you you’re always with me.”

Caitlyn’s breath caught sharply. Her fingers closed over the locket reverently before lifting it and delicately fastening the chain around her neck. Her eye glistened in the candlelight and the walls she kept so carefully faded revealing something like hope.

“It’s beautiful,” Caitlyn said softly.

“Thank you,” she added voice thick with emotion.

Silence settled around them like a warm blanket. Caitlyn reached beneath the table and despite a slight tremor Vi thought she saw pulled out a slender envelope. She slid it across to Vi reverence shining in her eyes as if she were entrusting her most precious secret.

Vi’s breath caught as she unfolded the paper slowly. A photograph lay inside, time-softened but alive with sunlight. Caitlyn laughed freely, vibrant and wild; Vi’s smile was light as they stood, captured in joy long before scars drew their lines. This was a moment unbroken by distance or pain when the world had still felt whole.

Caitlyn’s voice trembled breaking the hush.

“This is when I knew. The moment I realized I could never let you go. No matter what we faced. No matter the distance.”

A sharp ache corroded Vi’s heart. Memories rushed in like waves, lonely nights, silent battles and sacrifices made so love could survive. Her fingers curled tightly around Caitlyn’s under the table squeezing as though to speak every word she couldn’t say.

Caitlyn’s breath was warm against her hand steady and slow. Tears glistened behind their guarded eyes unspoken proof of how far they’d come, how fiercely they held on.

The bustling restaurant faded away leaving only the bubble of their shared light. Vi reached up and brushed a stray curl off Caitlyn’s face fingertips tracing the faint scar like a sacred map. Caitlyn leaned in and threaded her fingers through Vi’s hair pulling her close with patience born of years spent waiting.

Their lips met slowly, first soft and searching tasting the longing and relief of reunion. The kiss deepened a steady burn that melted years of absence away filling the space with whispered moans and mingled breaths.

Outside the city gleamed beneath an indigo sky. No distant stars could measure the quiet victories in this room, the sacrifices, the trust, and the choice to love again and again.
