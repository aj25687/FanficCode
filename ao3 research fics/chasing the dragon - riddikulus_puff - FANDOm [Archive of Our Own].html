chasing the dragon

riddikulus_puff

Summary:

Draco Malfoy was hiding something, a huge secret in his eyes. He believed that while it may have been obvious to those close to him — Theo, Blaise and at a stretch Pansy — but he wanted to reveal it to his new girlfriend, Hermione Granger, when he was ready to face the secret and bring it out into the open.

However, Hermione Granger knew something was going on with her boyfriend, and she would get to the bottom of it. She was always a know-it-all at the end of the day.

A one-shot for the 2025 HP Halloween Fic Fest

Notes:

  • In response to a prompt by Anonymous in the HP Halloween Fest 2025 collection.

prompt: dragon

(See the end of the work for more notes.)

Work Text:

━━━━━━━━━

Hermione Granger knew that something had felt weird when flying away from Gringotts Bank on the back of a silver coloured Ukrainian Ironbelly. The look from the dragon had struck her as the Golden Trio fell from the back of the dragon and dove into the freezing cold water, scalding through their bodies as the pain seared like electricity strikes.

And every night, Hermione was haunted by the face of the dragon.

Its pools of silvery grey stared into her soul that day the trio escaped the Bank, and she had felt that stare seep into her body every time she blinked, meeting the darkness behind her eyes.

And even now, the dragon’s face was stalking Hermione.

And it had been over a year since she last saw the dragon — last she had heard that specific Ukrainian Ironbelly had ended up in Romania with Charlie Weasley, which she hoped was true.

Now, however, it was her eighth year at Hogwarts — a do-over of what was supposed to be her seventh year — and Hermione was entering the year as Head Girl, but that was the only factor reminiscent of the old Hermione Granger. She had too many memories and nightmares from the Wizarding War that crowded over her and changed her in ways that she wasn’t prepared for.

However, Hermione had her boyfriend: Draco Malfoy.

And he had promised to protect her in any way he could, even if it was lying in bed with her and holding her while her body was wrecked with the onslaught of emotions. 

Hermione still struggled with handling her nightmares, and Draco promised to help her with them in any and every way.

But then there was one night in the middle of October when she had been left alone and her nightmares had attacked her stronger than ever before. Hermione was sweating, the beads rolling off her forehead as she searched for Draco, but her search came up empty. Her heart was beating out of her chest as she collected together some things - her warmest Muggle coat, some brown clunky boots and making sure her wand was in her pocket before she left the Head dormitories.

Hermione plucked at her heartstring magic, feeling along the taut hold as she followed the buzzing golden light, hoping that it would lead her to him. She remained hidden in the shadows, passing several prefects finishing their patrols as she was moved out of the castle and guided towards the Forbidden Forest.

And as her feet breached the entrance of the Forbidden Forest, the bright-looking moon shone down on her body as the bone-curdling screams hit her soul.

And Hermione recognised them instantly.

Her movements sped up as she sprinted through the Forbidden Forest until she came to an opening in the trees, which created a circle — the moon shining brightly upon the engravings on the ground.

And there in the middle was a naked, writhing Draco Malfoy.

His back was bent backwards as he screamed out in a blood-curdling scream as he writhed, his hands shuffling out as he searched for purchase to hold on to something. Draco was in torturous pain as a new form started to leave from Draco’s back, as his human form was overtaken and hidden as a large, blinding silver coloured Ukrainian Ironbelly stood where Draco once was.

Hermione stumbled backwards, stunned.

But, she couldn’t run away, and her feet were glued to the spot.

“Draco?” Her voice left her in a small, timid sound. Like she was a mouse as she stared at the creature that her boyfriend had become in front of her very eyes. She still couldn’t breathe as she blinked.

This was a dream.

It had to be a dream.

It wasn’t a dream. She thought as the dragon slowly turned its attention towards Hermione and snarled possessively as if recognising the small human in front of it.

He tilted his head to the side, like a dog, as he stared at the human — her curly hair calling out to him as her magic took on a swirling power as it reached towards him.
