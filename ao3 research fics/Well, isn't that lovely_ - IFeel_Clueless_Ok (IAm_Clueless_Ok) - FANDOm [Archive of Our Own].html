Harry POV:

He stares at the note.

You are welcomed back to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

He had been so focused he hadn't even gotten past the first line.

Back to school? Why? Could he survive that many people?

Most importantly, did he want to?

Sure he loved Hogwarts, it was the first place he called home.

But going back?

That was too much.

He stared at the paper before reading the regular note. He wasn't so spaced out that he got the gist there would be a whole eighth-year common room. He had also deduced with his amazing detective skills that this was an offer to all students, including Hermione Granger.

He loved Hermione, he really did. But her love for studying went unmatched with nearly everyone he knew. Her love for books was admirable, and he had certain subjects he loved learning as well, but knowing her, the moment she got that letter she would appear on his doorstep with Ron in her hand demanding they go back to complete their N.E.W.T.S.

He lifted his head to scan the room. It was still Grimmuald Place, not much had changed. There were new posters and the cabinets had been repaired. But the shelves still creaked under the old magic as he placed tea cup after cup on them. It was still a pain to get to the top floor; he never used them. Waking up was a repeat process of waking up, if he had been blessed with sleep, brushing his teeth, going downstairs, and taking care of the only thing he managed to keep semi-healthy with was a cactus that he was pretty sure he wasn't feeding right.

Going back to school would be good for him, not that he truly desired to be back with a bunch of kids who asked for his autograph and painful potions lessons as well as waking up at ungodly times. He waited and waited with his mug of tea in his hand not knowing what to do.

Not even an hour went by before Hermione showed up at his doorstep with Ron in her hand. 

"We NEED to go back to Hogwarts!" She shouted.

"Nice to see you too 'Mione" he grinned at her, or at least, that's what he hoped his expression looked like.

They shared quick hugs, seeing as they had just met not a week before.

"So, have you read the letter?" She stares at him expectantly after making her way to living room #1.

"Yes, I have. But before you go onto a whole tangent about going to finish our N.E.W.T.S., I have something to say," he starts before she can jump in. She begrudgingly waits for him and lets her previously tense posture loosen.

"I want to go. Well, I want to want to go. Look, I know we need to finish our education, but I don't feel ready. I know this is my only opportunity but think about it. We just spent a year trying to kill Voldemort, one of the most powerful wizards to have lived, and now here we are, not 6 months later being dragged back to school." He gives her a pained expression and he thanks God he was blessed with friends who could read between the lines and comprehend his facial clues.

"Yes Harry I hear you, and I won't pressure you to make a decision now. You don't even have to tell them you are going, although I don't advise you to. But I want you to know I will be going. I have to admit it will be weird without you two annoying me while I study, but I understand if the pressure will be too much," Sometimes he wonders what he did to deserve good friends like this.

"Yeah, mate. Not to put even more pressure on you, but I'll go if you go. Hogwarts wouldn't be the same without you. I'm up for whatever but don't make your decision based on me." Ron grins. 

If he could think, his brain would. The depressing half coma he had been in half the summer defiantly wasn't doing great for his mental health. And as much as it pained him to say it, he did miss the feeling of going to school. He had always heard about how graduating was one of the things people remembered for quite a while. And Voldemort had robbed him of having a normal life, or having what he truly wanted, happiness. But in that split second, his heart decided he wouldn't let Voldemort rob him of this.

"Well mate, I guess we'll have some potions studying to do."

Harry POV:

He sat at his desk, he had just spent the entire day staring at the paper, long after Hermione and Ron had left.

Why would you say you wanted to go?  Screamed one-half of his mind.

The annoying part of his mind answered with, It's because he wants to see-

But he quickly avoided and shoved those thoughts out of his mind.

His one brain cell continued to pester him telling him he should rethink or stick with his original decision. He had started to get a headache, so much so that he took a few Advils. After deciding it was slightly unhealthy to be sitting in this position for so long he got up and staked to the dresser, threw on an appropriate outdoorsy outfit, and made his way downstairs. He threw on a pair of shoes and came to the chimney.

After a lifetime's worth of decisions, he was still panicking at the prospect of going outside, let alone choosing where to go.

Diagon Alley? Muggle London? It should be obvious that he should go to the muggle world but recently, albeit begrudgingly, he had read at least a few paragraphs of the Prophet when Ron came around, apparently Harry Potter was, "off to some mystery place" or, "fighting a rising demon," and the best one yet, "hooking up with a French lover" and oh how he wishes he was hooking up with-.

No, absolutely not. Not right now.

Now, while he and Ron had shared a laugh at the titles, it struck something in Harry, he was destined to show off to the wizarding world, and people relied on him, even though he could barely rely on himself. Recently he forgot to have breakfast, lunch, and dinner and had spent most of the day watching TV and generally doing nothing. The more he thought about it, maybe it wasn't so irrational to think that he had some problems, other than the massive PTSD he had gotten from the war. Also, the a slight twitch in his hand when people slammed doors, but that was Vernons doing.

But unfortunately, he had a reputation to uphold, although why he still didn't know. So he waddled on over to the chimney and grabbed a handful of floo powder.

The prospect that anybody would like his autograph wasn't too unreasonable. They didn't know who he was. He hadn't done it all alone, his friends did most of the work with him only claiming fame. They didn't know that, they only read the news, the Prophet washing their minds. So every time people asked for a photo or an autograph, although awkward, he said yes, but this was different.

As he got out and made it onto the streets he was swarmed by so. many. people.

He had cameras, people, notebooks, reporters, books, and even cauldrons shoved into his face, somehow in that instant half of the wizarding world knew where he was going to be and decided this was the absolute perfect moment to get an interview or autograph. He shoved his way through the crowds politely asking people to move aside. "Mr Potter, do you know why there has been a recent spike of bear attacks in Canada?" "Mr Potter, what is your opinion on the law that outlaws babies from drinking potato juice in Albania?" "Mr Potter, what is it like living in a large mansion on the coast of Aruba?" "Mr Potter, when will you reveal who your French lover is, I've heard rumors that she isn't even French at all!" "Mr Potter, why did you defend Malfoy at his trial, you know he is a death eater?".

With every question more confusing than the last, he was almost relieved to hear a slightly normal question at the end. But still, it was about Malfoy. And right now he would very much appreciate not having the entire wizarding world know that he had a crush on his only remaining 'enemy', who people still generally had a disdain for.

Fortunately, he soon found peace as he made his way into a bookshop. Unfortunately, he hadn't brought the list so he couldn't see what he needed, thankfully as he scanned the room he spotted Neville Longbottom.

He stood there, slightly more muscular than before, in a soft sweater with a very calm outfit on. He looked more adult but still something so very Neville.

"Hey!" He called to his old friend as he made his way through the bookcases. "Oh, Harry!" He replied, before giving a soft hug. "You here for book shopping?" "Oh yeah!" He replied, trying to hide the fact that he had just been swarmed by at least half of the wizarding population. "But I forgot my list, any chance I could take a peek at yours?" He asked. Of course, Neville was going back. Neville had a certain love for school. Maybe not potions but he was pretty sure there wasn't a better Herbiologist out there than him.

Neville nodded his head before letting him take a peak at the list.

Waiting for Time Meldrid Hubble totally not a reference

Potions for the Advanced and Excellent Browning Clement, It was an honor to think Professor Slughorn thought so highly of us.

DAGA: Defence Against the Great Arts Wellington Brown

Plants and Fungus Galore, Oh What There is to Explore Varsington Weltch again totally not a reference

There were more, but that was all he saw on the list that was currently in Neville's hands. So he wandered around pretending he didn't have a care in the world searching for books until he made his way to the register where Neville waited for him. Together, they bought the books and sat at a small cafe at the back of the store, because all good bookstores must have cafes.

Neville talked about his Nan and her health, although they did pointedly ignore the subject of his parents preferring to keep the subject more lighthearted. Harry soon admitted the real reason he had entered was to leave the crowds, to which Neville responded with, "I guess people seem to like the fact one of the world's most famous wizards is finally making an appearance again,". He didn't take the comment to heart and decided to elongate the subject as they made up silly stories of where Harry might've been on his break.

After a good 2 hours, Neville had declared he ought to find his Nan who was probably wasting her money on more hideous clothes. Yet again amused by Neville's antics Harry left with a pleased smile across his face.

The day got even better because as he left the store only a handful of reporters remained.

He carefully dodged them as he made his way to the next store to get the necessities like a new quill, ink, and such. He decided that even though there were fewer reporters, it was a better idea to go with Hermione and Ron for the rest of his things. When he got back home nearly 3 hours had passed and he considered the day a success. 

As he fell asleep that night nightmares out of his mind, he slept peacefully until the morning.
