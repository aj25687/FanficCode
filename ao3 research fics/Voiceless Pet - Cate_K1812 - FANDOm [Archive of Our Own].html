Draco rolled his eyes, pushing Harry back a bit, and opening the door more to let Hermione enter, “Why don’t you come in and we talk about this over breakfast?” 

Hermione hesitated, but accepted the invitation.

While Draco led the way, Harry and Hermione started to argue over him having come here and just leaving, to which Harry shot back that he had written a note with his location on it and for them not to worry. Draco certainly preferred his mornings more calm and with less arguing, but overall his mood was way too good to get annoyed over it. 

As they reached the kitchen, Harry jumped ahead of him, “I’ll make the tea.”

Draco sighed and smiled, watching the black-haired bounce around the kitchen as if he had never been gone.

“Did you teach him cooking?” Hermione asked in a low voice, stopping next to Draco.

“Me? Oh no no,” he replied. “The only thing I can make is tea. I thought he learned it from you guys.”

Hermione shook her head, “At some point he just started cooking for Ron and me, but never mentioned where he learned it.”

“Hm… He just started cooking here as well,” Draco agreed. “And he does it well.”

“You guys know I can hear you, right?” Harry called back at them, placing the kettle on the stove. 

Draco just snickered a little, “We’re complimenting you. Why won’t you feel flattered about that?”

Harry pouted, pursing his lips as he strolled over to them, putting his arms around Draco’s shoulders, “Cooking is a basic skill. It’s more sad you can’t do it.”

“That’s why I have servants,” Draco just said smiling. 

Hermione loudly cleared her voice, attracting both their attention, “It’s nice to see you get along, but I can’t just approve of this.”

“Approval of…?” Draco asked.

“This… relationship situation,” she said.

Annoyed, Harry glared at her, pulling himself closer to Draco’s chest, “So? Why is the thing I want not okay? You always want me to make decisions on my own, but only when I say what you want to hear.”

“That’s not it!” Hermione denied. “But Malfoy is a recluse and criminal mastermind and you have therapy to attend to!”

“Actually, I agree,” Draco said, putting his hands on Harry’s hips.

“Excuse you?” Harry muttered, looking up at Draco.

“Granger is right,” Draco reiterated, “I’m not an upstanding citizen, despite paying taxes. Though being a criminal has nothing to do with me not sending you to therapy, since it was my idea in the first place.”

“You’d keep sending him to therapy?” Hermione asked. 

“It was my idea in the first place, and I can see betterment in his behaviour, which probably does come from therapy,” Draco said. “It’s hard to deny lovely, old Harry is still here.”

He gently stroked through Harry’s hair, brushing the small bouncy locks off his forehead. 

A stupid little smile showed on Harry’s face and he cuddled a little closer to Draco.

Hermione sighed, “Geez, you two… whatever.” She grumbled around, crossing her arms, “Fine, I guess! But listen! There are stipulations for this!”

“Hermione! You’re not my mother! Stop acting like it!” Harry barked at her, “I’m a grown man!”

“I’m listening,” Draco replied.

“One! Harry keeps going to therapy! Two! He has to come home at least three times a week. I can’t have him stay here unsupervised the entire time!” Hermione argued. “And three! You won’t have him involved in any of your unsavoury business!”

“Fine by me,” Draco agreed and held his hand out to her. “Let’s shake on it. You know I keep my promises.”

Hermione grabbed his hand and they shook on it. Just as Harry wanted to say something, the kettle whistled and he jumped away to take it off the flame.

Draco smirked, stroking through his hair and turning around, “Allow me to change real quick. My morning robe isn’t too suitable for guests.”

As he went away Hermione smiled softly after the blond, before walking over to Harry and helping him take out cups.

The more I see of the world, the more am I dissatisfied with it; and every day confirms my belief of the inconsistency of all human characters, and of the little dependence that can be placed on the appearance of merit or sense.”

Draco shut the book, put it on the night-stand, turned off the light and scooted down next to Harry. The other man grumbled a bit, and moved to get comfortable again. Draco smirked a little, waited for Harry to settle again and then brushing a gentle kiss on the top of his head.
