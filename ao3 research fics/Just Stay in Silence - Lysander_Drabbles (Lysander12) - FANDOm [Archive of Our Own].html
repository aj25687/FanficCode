Just Stay in Silence

Lysander_Drabbles (Lysander12)

Summary:

"I find you awake,
You tell me 'I'm sorry,'
With a tear spilling..."

Notes:

For the record, I 100% blame whoever posted that Portkey-Rebelde nostalgia mashup a few weeks ago for this. The nostalgia bug is contagious, dontcha know?

Gonna go ahead and set up a series for this, since RBD songs have suddenly renewed their residence in my head for the first time in over a decade and haven't paid a single cent of rent. Of course, I had to start with a sad song, so you can all share a bit of my pain.

I am neither JK Rowling nor RBD, and have no claim over any of the source material for this. I have no financial incentives nor benefits for writing or posting this.

Work Text:

"Te encuentro despierto,
Me dices 'lo siento,'
Con una lágrima derramas..."
- RBD, Solo Quedate en Silencio
(Translation in the story summary)

-----------------------------------------------

"Harry?" She entered the dark flat. It wasn't locked for her, thanks to Hermione's customized ward. Nevertheless, she was cautious, wand raised, anxious to know why he wasn't answering his mirror.

He was sitting there, staring at the floor, and didn't acknowledge her arrival.

"Why are you sitting in the dark?" She turned on the light, and a tear glinted at her from its perch on his cheek. "Are you alright?" She went and sat beside him, guessing that this had something to do with the war, which had ended nearly four months ago now.

He didn't respond, didn't acknowledge her.

"Harry, whatever it is, I'm here for you." She reached for his hand, but he pulled it away.

And then he turned to her, and she froze. The sadness in his expression was nothing compared to the guilt.

"I'm sorry, Ginny," he said quietly.

He didn't need to elaborate. She knew exactly what he was apologizing for, had seen it coming since before she even persuaded him to give their relationship another chance. She had seen the tension, the looks. It had been as clear as day that something had changed.

The last piece of the puzzle had fallen into place when Ron admitted to her that he had abandoned his best friends for a stretch of a few months while they were in hiding. He left them alone in the forest, with no one but each other to turn to.

She decided to not confront them, to take it in stride. What mattered was putting the terror and pain and grief behind her, and she needed Harry for that. She refused to acknowledge that she was setting herself up for more pain. She refused to acknowledge the reason why Hermione barely ever spoke to her directly anymore, and never looked her in the eye. She refused to acknowledge that Harry was always simply going through the motions with her, acting out the role she needed him to play.

And now she refused to acknowledge what needed to happen, at least for a few more minutes.

Instead, she wrapped her arms around his shoulders, burying her face in his shirt. He tensed for a long moment, and then returned the hug. But it was a tentative thing, offering no warmth or comfort. It was just an act, just him doing what she needed him to do, just as it had been all summer.

She detected the difference in his smell. He had showered afterwards, but with a different soap from the one he typically used.

She refused to acknowledge that, too.

Just for a few minutes.

The moment she started to pull away, his arms immediately dropped, his role fulfilled. He gazed at her face, his eyes still full of apology.

Apology, but not regret.

She managed a small, sad smile, then leant up and kissed the corner of his mouth. Then she stood, turned away, and left the flat, shutting the door quietly behind her.
