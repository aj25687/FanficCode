The images had come to him at innocuous moments, a glimpse here, a shimmer there. A flash of bloody hues, candles and mirrors and bodies in motion, seconds spiraling into stillness, the inevitability of destiny.

It was written in the clouds, in the stars. The sun would rise, the moon would fall, bodies would wither, and purity would conquer. As it always had.
