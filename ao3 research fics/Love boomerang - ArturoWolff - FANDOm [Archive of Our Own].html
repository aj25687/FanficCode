The prince asked then what he could do to regain her trust, but she was so deep in anger at the moment that all she did was bash him with the truth, that Ba Sing Se was taken because of what he did, that her mother was gone because of what his people did, and now that her father was at risk again thanks to him and his nation.

These words resonated in Zuko because he knew she wasn’t lying, but pondered on the events that lead to her mother’s death more than anything, feeling that if he could help her overcome her loss, she would trust him again.

That night he made the choice of talking to Sokka, obtain some information and maybe find a way to make amends. Unfortunately for him, he and Katara were already making out when he tried to reach him.
