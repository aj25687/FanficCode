Title: With these hands
Author: Katharos
Fandom: Avatar: The Last Airbender
Word Count: Exactly 100! I'm so proud

Notes: Ursa drabble

A nobleman’s daughter must be accomplished and so she learnt calligraphy, practising until her hands were deft and swift, until the sweep of her brush left only the ancient characters in its wake, glistening black and wet on the white paper.

She copied out the old stories; her life followed her lines, elegant and predictable. The Princess married the Prince.

Now she stands before her father-in-law in the black, shifting shadows cast by his fire, and remembers the girl who spilt ink across her paper and ruined her robes trying to clean it up.

Her hands are deft and swift.
