Chapter 1: Welcome back

Chapter Text

Growing up Elios’ parents told him he could achieve anything he would want to. That he could be whoever he wanted to be.

And Elio had believed them.

He had never doubted their support in anything because they had always been there for him. No matter what, they had his back.

They taught him everything he knows. No matter how intricate. Math's, physics.

Magic.

He could be anyone. And he could do anything.

Except one thing.

The thing he wants most.

 

"I want to go to the academy." Elio states sternly standing, arms folded in the doorway to the kitchen. It had been a perfect day. The sun was shining, his two younger siblings were playing outside, chasing around bugs. His parents were drying the dishes, while his baby sister was happily babbling in her highchair.

Until now.

Both his parents suddenly freeze. His papa's eyes go wide moving rapidly between him and his dad.

 

See, this isn't the first time Elio asked them about the academy.

"In piltover?" His papa had asked, eyes wide with shock the first time.

The second he had gotten nothing more than a stern "No." From his dad. No further details.

He knows this is a big ask. Not because they can't afford the application costs but because, well, Elios' parents don't have the best history with piltover.

 

That is to be expected when they both reside in piltover's history books now, under the section on what exactly happens when humans attempt to control things they shouldn't even be able to access.

 

"Honey I don't know about that. We can't just... we're legally dead." His dad V had stuttered.

"What If they find out." His papa J had agreed.

"They won't find out. I'll be careful." A bad defense given he's not the best of liers.

His dads only shook their heads in unison.

"Besides, you don't need those idiots in the academy. We can teach you more than they could ever know anyway. They're just a bunch of pretentious pilties." 

"Piltover isn't like that anymore-" he had tried but it came to nothing.

 

But three times the charm. And Elio isn't one to give up. His dads might not like it but he got his unwavering determination from them.

And he's certainly doing it justice.

"Do you want to walk all the way over there and back every day?" His papa nervously scratches his beard.

 

"I'll take a dorm room." Elio counters.

His papa blanks before exhaling loudly.

"Are you already old enough for that?" 

"It's just in piltover, it's not that far away. If anything happens at all, I'll come home."

"I just don't know how I feel about having you away from us for so long." His dad rubs his temple.

"It's five days. I'll be home on the weekends."

Elio claps his hands together.

"I promise."

"V?" His papa looks to his dad for an answer.

His dad pauses, fidgeting with his hands for a second.

"Elio, why don't you go out to your siblings for a moment. We need to talk." He eyes his papa.

"In private."

 

Elio sighs deeply and does as told, but not without making his displeasure known.

Ha makes sure to slam his door on the way out. Another no then. No bother. He'll try again.

He's not giving this up.

"Eli!" His sister screams excitedly as soon as she sees him.

"We caught a froggie! Look!" Astra squeals, holding up the slightly annoyed amphibian over her head. It lets out a tired unenthusiastic squaack.

 

"Wow! I thought you were catching bugs?" He laughs. 

"We were but then Atlas broke the net." She scowls at her twin brother.

"Did not!" Her twin brother frowns.

"Yeah you did!" She stops her feet into the grass.

Elio rolls his eyes smiling at their little squabble.

"Show me."

Atlas unhappily hands his big brother the little homemade butterfly net. The wooden handle is snapped right in half, the bottom half dangles loosely in the air, hanging on by a string of split wood.

 

Elio grabs a new stick up from the ground.

He cuts off the old broken remains of the stick and wraps a rope around the piece attached to the net. With a few tight knots he finishes his makeshift repair. He tugs the pieces a few times to make sure they are tightly attached before handing the net back to his brother.

"Woah!" Atlas gasps at the unimpressive repair.

"You can fix anything!" Astra beams, finally letting the frog out of her gasp. It hops up onto the wooden porch.

 

It's nothing special really. But still it feels nice to make his siblings happy with something so simple. Elio has always been the one in the household who would try to fix all sorts of broken things. 

When he was their age he would take apart old broken appliances and try to make sense of their inner workings.

Thankfully instead of punishing him, his parents had recognized his curiosity and encouraged it. They taught him how to fix almost anything.

And Elio is more than grateful for that.

 

He loves his parents. And his siblings. And this house but he doesn't want to stay here forever.

 

Elio sits down on the wooden doorsteps to the deck. The amphibian doesn't acknowledge him when the wood creaks next to it. It simply sits. What else would it do after all?

They both watch the twins run around in the grass without a care in the world.

He can't stay here. 

Time only goes by faster the older you get. Before he knows it his baby sister will be playing with them in the garden.

 

Suddenly he hears the door behind him open. The wood creaks in the rhythm of his dads footsteps. He sits down next to him.

"Elio." His dad says his name like a question.

"Hmm?"

"Do you really want this?" He asks. His hair is now undone, no longer in the loose bun.

"I do. I have wanted this for a while now. It's really important to me. I know you think it's stupid-"

"It's not stupid." His dad interrupts him, fidgeting with his wedding ring.

"We've been talking, Jayce and I. When I was your age-"

"Oh gods-" Elio lets his body flop back onto the wooden deck ground. Not this again.

 

His dad chuckles.

"Just listen. When I was your age, I wanted to go to the academy so bad. To me, it was the most important thing in the world. I had taken on my first job to save up money for the application and I worked so hard." His dad pauses, he takes a deep breath.

"And then?"

"I didn't get in. They stopped me on the bridge and refused me entry to the academy grounds."

That's news to him. Elio sits back up.

 

"Wait I thought- how did you get in then?"

"My mom made me a fake uniform. And I just walked right in there. I pretended I just belonged there."

"And that worked?" Elio raises an eyebrow.

 

"For a while, until Heimerdinger found me."

"Oh." Elio knows the rest of the story now.

"And then he made you his assistant."

His dad puts a hand on Elios shoulder.

"Son, if you really want this, I can't stop you from it. You've got my stubbornness, that's for sure. I know you'll find a way." He smiles.

Wait, is this happening?

His papa is suddenly standing behind them.

He kneels down with a gentle smile on his face, genuine and warm. His wrinkles only enhance it.

 

"I would just prefer you do it with us by your side." Elio feels like his heart has stopped.

His papa holds out his hand. On his palm lies a silver necklace, attached to it is a shiny blue runestone, almost exactly like the one in his papa's wrist. Only this one has an unfamiliar rune carved into it.

His papa puts the necklace around Elios neck and closes the clasp at the back.

 

Elio takes the runestone into his hand.

He looks back at his dad, tears now stinging his eyes.

 

"We'll always be right here."

Ah Piltover.

Despite how much they love to claim they have changed, visually it's still the same golden ostentatious city of the rich.

The most glaring example of the performative activism are hexgates, which still stand right where his parents had built them, only now devoid of energy. Only a testament. A memorial of sorts or maybe a reminder of their history.

But to piltover it's nothing more than another way to make money.

A thing for people to take photos of and oogle at.

 

Elio rushes through the crowd of anxious students huddled in the academy halls. Most of them, confused by the sheerly absurd layout of the academy, are still trying to find their classrooms.

Today is the day. 

His first day. The entrance exam was a breeze for him so if that's anything to go by this is going to be great.

He makes it to his classroom with about eleven minutes to spare. Better early than late.

 

He takes a seat next to a window two rows from the professor's podium.

The only other people already in the room with him are two girls in the front row whispering to each other and a brown haired guy sitting right in front of him, quietly humming while he writes something in his notebook. He doesn't pay Elio any mind at first, only glancing at him briefly when he enters the room before his eyes snap quickly back to his notebook.

That is until he notices that Elio has sat down behind him.

 

The boy in front of him suddenly spins around in his seat to face him. He has thick square glasses perched on his nose, making his green eyes appear slightly bigger than they probably are. His hair is straight but fairly messy, a few strands stand straight up like excited alien antennae.

He's wearing a green academy hoodie, piltover's academy logo slapped right on the front. 

Though the design doesn't look like the one on the shirts they sell now. That and the fact that the color looks quite washed out hints at the fact that the hoodie is a rather old one.

 

"I like your hair!" The boy beams. 

"Thank you." Elio smiles. The first time he's gotten a compliment from a stranger. He runs his fingers through his dark wavy hair.

He does have a few white strands peeking through here and there, sticking out quite drastically from the dark brown majority. All of his siblings have them as well. 

"Traces of your dad's magic" His papa would say. Though his dad seems to have more mixed feelings about them.

It's good to know that other people think positively of them.

“They're natural actually. It's genetic.”

“That's so cool I've only ever heard about that before.”

 

Right, time to make friends, from his image, and make connections with new people.

"I'm Elio." He introduces himself, hopefully not too awkwardly. 

"No last name." He adds. Obviously if he gives out his full name he would be in deep shit. Not just from his dad's but probably everyone in piltover. Gods can only imagine if the piltover council found out, the two creators of hextech are not only alive but have children as well.

 

Elio extends his hand to the boy. A normal piltovan gesture his papa had taught him. You say your name, hold your hand out, take the other person's hand and you shake it.

"Flint Freesia." The other boy smiles at him wide and welcoming. He takes Elios hand and gives it an excited shake. A lot stronger than the one he practiced with his dads.

It's good to put a name to the face.

 

"Where are you from?" Flint asks, pushing up his glasses after the shaking pushed them down his nose.

"Uh-" good question actually. Dad V is from the fissures and his papa from piltover, (even though he wasn't actually born there and would definitely give him the whole story of their travel to piltover and the snowstorm again.)

But Elio wasn't raised in any of those places really. He's only really been to piltover to pick up groceries. He's barely seen the top levels of zaun.

 

So what does he say? He's studying abroad? He lives in the woods? He said he doesn't have a last name and that's usually only a thing in the undercity so...

Shit the guy us still waiting for an answer. Talk. Say something, open your big dumb idiot mouth.

 

"I'm actually from out of piltover, well, near piltover. It's kind of a rural-"

"Cool!"

Flint's mind seems to be somewhere else.

"Do you have an accommodation buddy?" He asks quickly, switching topics with a sense of urgency to his voice.

"A what?" 

"Do you not have a dorm room here?"

Oh right, does this guy already consider Elio his friend? And a close enough one to share an apartment with? Does this normally happen so fast?

His papa had an academy dorm too, he shared it with this roommate that he called his “academic rival”. His dad would always laugh when he spoke of him.

What was his name again? Not important. Elio shoves the thought away.

So maybe Flint just doesn't care too much about who he lives with. Maybe he's the only one left without a roommate and that's why he's asking him.

 

"I am planning to register. I haven't done it yet."

Flint springs up.

"I have but I don't have a roommate yet. You wanna share my apartment?"

Wow, okay, uh.

"Sure." Why not right. Make connections. Be more social and all. That's what he wanted after all. And a good scientist knows to take a risk every now and then.

"Awesome. You're really saving my ass here." Okay that was fast. But that's a good thing, right? It means the guy likes him, right?

 

Slowly as the time passes the other students trickle in one by one until the classroom is full.

 

"Good morning everyone." A tall broad shouldered man enters the classroom and drops a stack of books off on the professor's podium. He looks friendly enough. His long black hair is tied back in a relaxed ponytail, a short goatee rests on his chin. 

"I will be your engineering professor for this first year."

That seems about right.

"You can call me mister Nova or professor Nova. Whatever you prefer."

 

He pulls a thick folder out from under his arm. 

"Now first things first I want to get the accommodations sorted..."

Professor Nova explains what Elio already knows to the classroom.

 

"Most of you will likely be home on the weekends and during the holidays, but if you wish you can always stay here. Visits from friends and family are welcome but if any of your parents are planning to visit you, we would prefer you to notify the accommodation staff beforehand."

That's not happening, Elio scoffs. If his parents get found on academy grounds that would... That would be really bad.

 

"If anyone has any questions you can always ask me during before or after your lectures. I am also here on Fridays, if I'm not in a lecture I'm usually in my office, the room number is in your booklet. If you need help otherwise professor Heimerdinger is always on the academy grounds and available."

Wait. That can't be right.

 

"I thought he died?" Elio whispers to Flint.

"Yeah everyone thought that but turns out he was just one some life changing travel out of piltover."

"Huh." So his parents' old mentor would also be his. How odd. He wonders if they'll be glad to hear he's alright.

 

"Now, with all that aside. I welcome you to your first lecture at poltovers renowned academy."

The next five hours of different lectures pass by in what feels like a minute.

It's crazy. This feeling. He's finally doing what he's always wanted. 

Once the school day is over elio makes his way over to the accommodations for the first time. It takes him a while to find his apartment number since the layout of the buildings in piltover are a sheer labyrinth but he ended up finding it nonetheless.

 

Time to move in and be independent. How exciting.

For an accommodation the apartment is surprisingly large and fancy. Lots of space in the living room. A tiny kitchen. A bathroom with a bathtub and shower separately to his surprise.

Elio drops his suitcase off in his room and begins to unpack only shortly before Flint stumbles in through the door.

He's carrying nothing more than a rather stuffed backpack slung over his right shoulder. It looks uncomfortably heavy and is probably, definitely doing all sorts of damage to his back. His papa would scold him.

No suitcase.

 

"Is that all or is this your second trip?" He asks jokingly. Though looking at Flint's bed onto the other side of the room the latter is certainly impossible. The bed has no sheets on it yet, the shelves bare, the closet empty.

He doesn't want to pry and he didn't bring a lot either but one backpack seems.

"Nah, this is all I've got."

Oh alright then.

Flint opens the zipper and flips the bag over, proceeding to dump the continents of it onto his bed. 

They fall out with an audible thud as a tightly crammed together ball of fabric.

 

"I won't take up too much room, don't worry."

"And oh I almost forgot, here." Flint excitedly shuffles through his stuff and pulls out a dark red mug, still in the cardboard packaging. He hands it out to Elio with a wide toothy grin.

"I got us matching mugs because we're buddies now. And for sharing the apartment."

"Oh thank you. That's so nice." The color matches Elios' jacket, it's his favorite. A lucky guess.

 

Elios eyes trail back to the ball of clothes and little piled on Flint's bed.

"Is that really everything you brought?" It comes out before he can stop it.

"I don't mean that in a judgemental way I just..." he immediately tries to trail back.

"Pff no it's fine," Flint waves it off.

"We just don't have a lot of money. We had to save up a bit for the exam."

Oh.

"I can lend you some of my stuff if you need it."

"You would do that?”

“Of course we're study buddies after all.”

"Do you have siblings?" Flint asks eventually while they unpack together.

"Well we're mostly independent. We grow our own food and share a lot of things. But my papa also..." made hextech. Was a counselor. Lead a war against his dad.

Flint finishes hanging up his shirts, there weren't a lot of them, and looks over at Elio.

"Ooh, which one?"

"Uh-" shit, think. Big companies? Big successful piltover companies. Elio can feel the cogs turning in his head. The “Uh” currently coming out of his mouth only getting longer and longer and more awkward.

"The Talis forge." He blurts out and instantly wants to slap himself.

Why in the world was that the only thing he could come up with.

 

It's not wrong, Jayce Talis certainly did work in the Talis forge. And the Talis company did experience quite a boom during hextechs development.

"What! That's awesome! Did he ever meet Jayce Talis himself?"

"No idea." Elio lies, talking way too fast for his own liking.

 

"My mom met his assistant once." Flint claims.

"Who?" Elio asked genuinely.

"You know who,... the herald." Flint whispers. Elio instinctually flinches. Right, that's what his father is known for now. A quite grim legacy isn't it. To only be known for the worst part of your life.

 

"But before he was the herald.” Flint clarifies. “They met at a progress day presentation held by Heimerdinger, when he was still working for Talis."

"Working with Talis, he was his partner not his assistant." Elio subconsciously corrects him.

"Is that true? I never knew that. Anyway so this guy-" 

This guy. Urgh.

"His name was Viktor."

 

"Right, whatever mister smarty pants." Flint chuckles. We'll at least he's open to learning. 

"So she was at this presentation, it was on the plaza, and this other guy came up to her and kept getting all up in her business. Just a really slimy guy, you get what I'm saying? Wouldn't stop trying to touch her. Made her really uncomfortable,"

"Mmm gross."

"Exactly. So then this Viktor guy noticed and apparently smacked him right in the shins with his cane. Really whacked him." 

Elio can't help but let out a laugh.

That does sound like something his dad would do no questions asked.

 

"Small world huh?"

"It sure is."

Chapter Text

When Elio and Flint enter the classroom in the morning a whole bunch of students are already huddled around the professors podium. Whispering at each other seemingly gathered around something apparently very interesting. 

 

Flint quickly rushes up to the group without a second thought and sticks his head into the crowd.

"Oh my god, he was such a dweeb. Elio you need to see this." He shouts.

Elio sighs and looks over the other students shoulders. He can finally see what they are so fascinated by.

It's a yearbook. A pretty old one. The paper is slightly yellowed by its own age. The edges of the paper crumpled. 

It looks well loved, his papa would say.

 

As in any other regular yearbook, multiple rows of students' pictures are depicted on the pages. But the one the students are pointing to and giggling is a familiar one.

A familiar blocky face with a long dark ponytail hanging over his shoulder.

 

Dimitri Nova. It reads right next to... oh god. That's- Elios mind blanks.

"Holy shit, he shared a dorm with the hextech guy! Could you imagine." One of the students says mouth hanging open.

Jayce Talis, his papa. Looking proudly into the camera. Smiling slightly awkwardly, tooth gap and eyebrow scar and all.

"Haha that's crazy." Elio fake laughs, attempting to fit into the group.

So his papa's academic rival is their professor. It really is a small world after all.

 

"I wonder what he was like?" One of his classmates swoons, twirling her hair.

"I bet he was a lady's man, even back then." Elio rolls his eyes.

"My mom met him once. She said he was really nice but super defensive about his assistant?" Another student claims.

Elio can't let it slide.

"Do you mean their actual assistant or his partner-" Elio prepares himself to start defending his dad again, but this time he is prematurely cut off.

 

"Yoink" their professor suddenly appears behind them and snatches the yearbook out of the students grasp.

"Now which one of you poked around my personal belongings?" He scolds them sarcastically.

"Hey come on, you just left it on the podium." The thief defends herself, folding her arms over one another.

"You didn't tell us you knew Talis! That's a big deal."

Unsurprisingly mister Nova rolls his eyes at them.

"Well my students do not need to know all the details of my personal life."

Professor Nova laughs.

 

"At least tell us what he was like." One student pouts.

"Yeah we demand knowledge!" Another yells.

 

Professor Nova sighs, putting the yearbook under his arm. 

"He was a smart guy. I would say we were good buddy's."

Can't say his papa would agree with the ladder but sure. Good to know that the disdain wasn't mutual. That only makes this funnier though.

Elio gets to their apartment first that day since Flint still has his afternoon PE classes. 

It's an odd feeling, being completely alone in their shared accommodation for the first time.

The quiet is nice, don't get him wrong, but being completely alone is a new feeling for him. 

Growing up he's always had at least one other person around. If not his papa, then his dad. And on the rare occasion that they were both out of the house at the same time, he had his younger siblings to take care of.

In order to keep his mind busy he starts to clean up a bit. Dusting the shelves, wiping the counter top, refolding his bedsheets once, then twice, then again because it just didn't feel right the last time.

 

By the time he's done he becomes too aware of his stomach's emptiness.

Too tired to start cooking at this time of day, he takes the tupperware of frozen soup his papa had made for him a few days before out of the freezer and plops it into a small pot to heat up.

 

As the block of ice slowly melts the door clicks open.

"Hey you're-" Elio looks over to his roommate, who just entered their apartment. He stops smiling. His big brother instincts take over.

"What happened to you?" 

Flint's pants are ripped open at the front revealing his bloody scraped up knees. His face is cut up and covered in streaks of dirt.

 

"Oh I tripped, I'm fine really." He lies, badly. Elio can see it in his eyes.

"Somehow I don't believe that." He frowns at his friend.

Flint sighs. "These two guys shoved me during the endurance run." He admits.

"But it's nothing, I'll live." He tried to joke but it's not the time for that right now.

Elio turns off the stove.

"We should disinfect them." He states looking closer at Flint's knees. The scrapes are fairly deep into the skin.

 

His parents had drilled this into him. Infections can get really bad. If you can prevent them you have to.

His papa had to learn that the hard way during his time in the ravine.

"I'll get you some stuff, sit down."

"You don't have to-"

"Sit."

Flint sits.

Elio retrieves a damp towel as well as some disinfectant spray from the bathrooms first aid cabinets.

 

He gently wipes down the other's face without even thinking about it.

It's a familiar scene for him. His little brother is a climber, he'd often end up with a few scrapes here and there, that's sort of unavoidable. He'd always come to Elio first because he didn't want to get yelled at by their parents and get the ol "I told you not to climb on that" again.

And Elio would clean up the wounds and disinfect them before wrapping them up. Just like he's doing right now.

He's never thought anything of it. After all it was just his little sibling.

 

But right now he is becoming all too aware of how close he is to his friend's face. And just how red that face is suddenly getting.

And how weird he feels all of a sudden.

Elio finishes bandaging the scrapes and puts the sanitizer aside.

 

Flint quickly thanks him, avoiding eye contact for some reason.

Did he make him uncomfortable? He wouldn't want that. 

“Have they done this to you before?” He asks. Flint doesn't answer.

Best to just change the subject.

"Right, I was going to heat up some soup if you want some we can share."

"That would be nice." Flint smiles still looking elsewhere. His cheeks are still as red as before but he looks a bit more relieved at least.

"No, they did have an assistant but it wasn't Viktor, it was a woman. Her name was Sky Young. She was from the undercity." Elio explains while they sit on their beds, eating the now hot soup out of their seperate bowls..

 

"So how did their fude happen then? I always thought he was probably just jealous of Talis for taking up all the spotlight." Flint asks between spoonfuls.

"No he wasn't. They just had differing opinions on what hextech should be used for. And then that..."

He can not tell this guy about the necromancy. The general public doesn't even know about that. Although for some reason the thought is enticing. To tell someone. To have someone he can trust enough to tell him his real story. Their real story.

"...kind of escalated."

 

"How do you know so much about him?" Flint raises an eyebrow.

"I just think Viktor was an interesting guy." His lies feel increasingly guilty for a reason he can't place.

"You're not one of those people who worship him right?" Flint drops the question like a rock on a glass panel.

"What? No! What?" Elio nearly chokes.

Flint puts the bowl down on his nightstand. He slaps his hands together like he's about to tell a story.

"There's a cult. They call themselves the followers of the glorious herald."

Elio swallows.

"I thought they all died." That's what his parents told him at least. Viktor had evolved them under the manipulation of the hexcore only made worse by the grief of their fractured partnership. And the only way to set them free in the end was to liberate their souls from their evolved puppet bodys. 

That's the story.

Can they possibly still be around?

 

"Oh no they did but this is a recent thing. These people never met him; they're just obsessed with the guy. Think he was misunderstood." Oh?

"Huh I didn't know that." His dad is not going to like that. Knowing there's still people out there worshiping him like a god. He would only spiral. Maybe he should keep this to himself.

"They want to "shed themselves of their mortal forms". Believe that the glorious evolution was a good thing. That the herald was some godlike being sent by Janna herself to free them all."

"That's... worrying."

"Yeah you're telling me. I saw one of them on the market last week. He tried to hand me a pamphlet."

 

Elio has to sit with that for a while.

 

"I don't understand how people can sympathize with him." Flint eventually breaks the silence.

Elio gives him a lopsided frown.

"People sympathize with Jinx." It's a bad response, he knows this.

"Jinx was a revolutionist. She helped lead zaun to independence. 

The herald wanted to essentially eradicate all of humankind. He was like cartoonishly evil."

 

"He didn't really want to though. He thought he was doing good."

"Uh oh. You're not one of them are you?" Flint leans in.

"It's important to analyze history and understand where he was coming from in order not to repeat it and prevent it from happening." Elio defends himself.

 

"Oh you know too much about this guy you would have taken the pamphlet."

"Shut up!" Elio laughs and throws a pillow at him.

 

÷0÷0÷0÷

 

It's the middle of the night.

Elio can't sleep. His mind is too busy still processing the events of today.

A little sigh and roll over on Flint's bed alerts him that his friend is experiencing much of the same.

"Flint, why did you ask me to be your roommate? I mean you didn't even know me"

 

Flint rolls onto his side.

"I really need this apartment. My parents... we don't have a lot of money. If I could just finish my studies and get a good job I could help them out. I stay here on the weekends, you know.

Because if I stay at home I'm just more work for them." 

Flint confesses. He pauses.

Elio doesn't push him. He waits for him to continue on his own.

"I had this other guy that said he would share with me only to then back out a day before the time was up to register them. Thought it was funny to see me desperate."

Holy shit some people truly are awful.

 

"And then you showed up. And you looked cool and nice and I don't have anyone else. You might have noticed I'm not too popular with the other guys."

 

"I think you're cool."
