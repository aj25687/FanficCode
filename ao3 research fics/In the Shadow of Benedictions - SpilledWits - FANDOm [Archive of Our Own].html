In which Sebastian admits a rather inconvenient truth after getting drunk off of fire whiskey, while also running after Theo Devlin as she dives headfirst into more reckless shenanigans.

Notes:

Notes:
Mild Spice served on the side for this chapter, but enjoy a main helping of emo love confessions and angst instead ;)

Instead of recapping the main story quests, I tried to fill in the gaps of what would happen in between them (with some dramatic liberties, of course), so you’ll find this is set up in a series of breaks.

For reference, breaks occur after In the Shadow of the Mine, and then again after Logdok’s Loyalty.

 

Order of the Devlin x Sallow one shot series:

 

1) Bad Habits
2) Snake Bites
3) In the Shadow of Benedictions

 

As always, characters are aged up (18+) for the purpose of these FFs.

Work Text:

[ben·e·dic·tion]
noun
“the utterance or bestowing of a blessing, especially at the end of a religious service.”

 

“You seem especially cheerful today.” Ominis chided as he found Sebastian brooding on the bleachers of the empty quidditch pitch.

Sebastian barely gave a sign of acknowledging the sarcasm in his friend's comment as he approached, keeping his back turned and eyes focused on something much farther away. He grimaced. For a blind bloke, Ominis was always adept at picking up on his body language whether he wanted him to our not. Observant bastard.

“Aren’t I always?” Sebastian’s response was muted, almost self deprecating.

Ominis sighed. Sebastian had been like this ever since his return last night from the mountain past the South Bog. He wouldn’t tell him much of what happened that night…only that both him and Theo had come across some trouble with more Rangrok loyalists, and he thought that they might be getting closer to find a cure for Anne.

Ominis knew he wasn’t telling him everything. He knew his friend’s mood’s to be fickled, but lately the rollercoaster of his emotions had been quite turbulent.

And ultimately Sebastian never showed his true hand until he was sure he’d get what he wanted…or at least it was very hard to get him to do so since their most recent disagreements.

Of all the moods Sebastian had, Ominis would have thought that finding a cure for Anne would have pleased him, instead Sebastian had become so withdrawn, with his brows constantly pulled together in troubled concentration.

He knew that Anne had never been a distant thought from Sebastian’s mind since he met him, but since Theo…Sebastian had been different.

He wasn’t just a boy preoccupied with his thoughts. He was all consumed by them.

Which could only mean one thing.

“So…are you?”

Sebastian turned his head at Ominis’s question, stilling just slightly.

“Am I what , Ominis?”

The question coming was one that Sebastian himself had already started to fear for a few weeks now.

Ominis repeated himself.

“In love with her, Sebastian… Are you in love with her?”

Though Ominis couldn’t see him, he swore he could sense the pounding of his friend’s heart, the rush of blood at being forced to address an inconvenient truth.

Instead, it was eventually the arrogant scoff that followed.

“Even if I was,” he responded with deliberate slowness, “I won’t make that her problem.”

He didn’t have the luxury to…and neither did Anne.

Theo stared out into the Great Lake. For a moment, it felt good to take in the stillness of the moment and the peace of a sunrise. Winter had finally settled, as the briskness of the season nipped in the wind, she brought her leather coat closer, taking its high collars up to guard her from the gusts. There was already a lot ahead of her today, so she thought it best to go back to the riding britches and the heavy cloak. And no more skirts.

Despite her layers though, she still felt a chill in her bones.

The hues of purple crept over the valley, and she stayed watching long after they faded to the deep blues of morning.

As the sun rose over the castle and all it’s inhabitants, and it reminded her that although she felt so lonely at times, she wasn’t anymore…and that out there in that dawn waited her friends. Ominis. Sebastian.

Friends?

Her traitorous mind flashed to the broad freckled hands that had spread across her abdomen in fleeting touches a few nights ago. The hands that handled his wand alongside her just as quickly, just as relentlessly. The hands that also pointed towards her in such a commanding way last night.

“You have overcome everything. You can wield this. You have the ability.”

His voice still rang in her ear from the night before. She had heard his words…understood his pleading…but hadn’t managed to correct him on a simple secret she held close to her heart.

There was one thing she hadn’t been able to overcome at all. The fact that as much as she had vowed they wouldn’t, her emotions were inevitably starting to get in the way of her mission. In the way of what Fig needed from her, what the Keepers had entrusted her to understand.

But in between that, Sebastian had kept her steady. Pushed her to keep going. Challenged her to see the possibilities. Looked at her with something undecipherable in his eyes right before he would kiss her long and slow. Somehow, he made her feel invincible when they were together.

Somehow, she was starting to care for him.

And that’s why it was getting increasingly harder to say no to him, even though she knew she should.

As the sun’s heat increased as much as it could over the snow covered mountain range and valley, it’s rays stretched across the lake, but despite the way the sun burned relentlessly…it’s light would never reach the lake’s dark and murky bottom. The secrets that it held would lay there forever.

It’s depths felt so unfathomable from where she was. She wondered what it would be like to dive deep down and get far away from all of it - the keepers, the triptych…her thoughts. To be yet another secret among many.

She cursed. Plucking up yet another rock, she chucked it a particularly far distance across the water.

It was nearly evening as Theo got ready to meet Ominis before tonight’s nightly feast.

She had to see him before finding Logdok…as she hadn’t been able to find Sebastian all day, she had assumed he was avoiding her. Which he tended to do after their larger disagreements…. and when she was stubbornly doing something he thought she shouldn’t.

Then, she startled as her owl, Hewin, suddenly came crashing into her dorm room, making angry calls at her until she finally noticed the small bit of parchment tied to his talons.

Her brow furrowed as she unrolled it, as Hewin’s feathers ruffled in satisfaction at the removal of his task. She gave him a distracted pat on the head as she cursed reading the message:

“Theo -

Thought you outta know, Sebastian is here at Hogshead…has been since early this afternoon apparently. I’ve been told he has been reigning terror on the house- elves and convinced them to give him copious amounts of fire whiskey before I arrived for the night shift. I know you two are close…may want to come retrieve him before Professor Weasley finds out one of her students is decidedly plastered…

-Sirona”

She hissed. That damned fool.

She immediately rushed out of the common room, sprinting up the staircase that reached up under the Great Hall.

Out of all the idiotic things…he knew I was seeing Logdok tonight…and he picks a time like this to go get drunk at Hogsmeade?!

She was nearly breathing fire by the time that she got to the Viaduct courtyard that she didn’t notice Ominis calling her.

“Theo! Did you hear me? I’ve been shouting your name. ” He ran up to her, worry etched upon those cloudy searching eyes.

She sucked in a breath - of course. She had told him to meet him up here…and Ominis, being the attentive friend that he was, had been waiting.

He didn’t need to hear the panic in her breath before knowing something was wrong.

“Sebastian’s done something stupid again, hasn’t he?” He intoned flatly before she could even speak.

“Of course he has. When is he not doing something stupid. Sirona’s informed me he decided it was a good idea to go get plastered at Hosghead when I need his help to find Logdok tonight.” She snapped out.

Mildly, Ominis found he was not so surprised, knowing the conversation they had shared earlier that day.

“Would you like me to go with you to find Logdok instead?” He immediately offered, but Theo just shook her head, smiling slightly. He was always looking out for her, and it endeared him to her.

“I can’t ask you to do that Ominis, I know…I know how you feel about the whole situation. I wouldn’t want you to get in the middle of all this.”

Again - little did she know he already was.

“I just don’t want you there alone, Devlin.” He was worried about her.

She just gave him a confident smile…always putting on a show for others that all was under control.

“You’re a good friend Ominis. Thank you - but I’ll be fine…or I will be. After I give Sebastian a piece of my mind.”

Ominis sighed, absently observing the odd love language that had been established between his two friends. They were both impossibly stubborn.

“Well...alright….at least please kick his ass for me when you get there.”

Sebastian knocked back another shot of fire whiskey, after terrifying Sirona’s poor houself for more until he had just brought him the whole damn bottle.

Merlin knew he needed it after today. He wanted to put thoughts of Theo and his emotions completely out of his mind. He wanted to be one step away from Obliviating himself…and this would be the way to do it.

Sirona just eyed him in warning.

“Sebastian Sallow, you know I love to see you. But not like this. You better slow down over there or you’ll drink yourself right under the table.”

He gave her a loathing smile, scoffing as if it was obvious.

“That’s the goal Sirona. That’s the bloody goal.”

The barkeeper just shook her head. She had known Sebastian long enough to know he had his demons. After watching him and his sister grow up only to lose their parents…and now the illness. She had always looked out for the boy where his Uncle would not…but it had been time to call in reinforcements. The door crashed open, and Theo arrived just in time with her eyes fixed right upon Sebastian’s Sallow suddenly ashen face.

“Bloody hell.” He muttered under his breath, as his smug smile was wiped clean off of his face.

Sirona made herself scarce quickly. This wasn’t going to be pretty.

Theo’s eyes narrowed on him as she charged across the room, to the way his languid figure leaned against the bar barely able to sit up straight.

“Sebastian Sallow. I thought I’ve seen you at your worst but I have to admit - you look like absolute dog shit right now.”

His smile clumsily twitched in ire.

“Ah yes. Just the lovely little Hufflepuff I was trying so diligently to avoid. Thank you for reminding me how much of a mess I am…” He let out a broken laugh as he avoided her eyes before finally meeting them. She could see the flush in his face, no doubt courtesy of the whisky.

“Messy, messy. And…. just take a stab at who’s fault that might be.” He took another pointed look at her before he swigged the whisky again. She snagged it out his hands and he looked at her as if he’d been personally affronted.

“You can’t possibly be implying what I think you are.” Her eyes narrowed in on him, the hazy look in his eyes as he regarded her. For some reason, she swore she saw fear in them.

“What are you so afraid of Sebastian?” She leaned her elbow against the bar she her head inclined into his space, forcing his gaze back to her.

“Well…see the funny thing is…it used to be nothing.” He muttered in annoyance, smiling cruelly at her, “Until you came around…complicating everything like you do.”

Her eyes widened.

“ME? Sebastian - how? I’ve done nothing but try to help you. To try and help Anne - I’ve done everything for you.”

He winced again. Hating himself even more for what he had to say to her. What he should say to her. How they needed to stop this before their emotions got the best of them.

But instead, his eyes regarded the flush in her cheeks.

Instead, he chose to see the look in her eyes that reminded him that she really knew him better then anyone else.

Instead…he found himself wanting those looks directed towards him forever, whether she was speaking sweet nothings to him, or biting his head off in the middle of Hogsmeade.

That damned fire-whisky.

“Do you ever want to just…escape?” His words slipped out of his mouth before he could stop them, watching her features as they caught her off guard. He liked doing that. He took a strand of her hair and curling it in his fingers lazily.

“Maybe we could get away from here. Get a little cottage on the coast, get away from it all. Away from Keepers…away from curses. Have a couple of little witch’s and wizards of our own one day. Give them everything we never had and all that…”

His smile was distant and far away, but something deep within his gaze was burning as she held her breath at his confession.

“…What a cozy little life we could have together.” He muttered as he regarded her.

She pulled away from his grasp. From his words.

“Stop taunting me Sebastian. You’re being cruel.”

All at once he looked crest-fallen. Helpless.

“I wish I was, Devlin.” He groaned.

There. It was proof. Of how hard everything had suddenly become, because of her.

She swallowed. Of course he would say these words to her drunk. After everything they had fought about last night. After his refusal to help Logdok. He was so manipulative to fight with her endlessly, only to then offer her a vision of what had always been out of reach for both of them - a family. A future. Together. It hurt her enough to hope for it. She wouldn’t let him toy with her like this.

“We…are not the cozy type of people, Sebastian.” She finally replied.

His gaze dropped as he smiled sadly at her words.

“No…I suppose you’re right.” His eyes found hers again, some undeciphered emotion brimming under those golden lashes brushing his freckled face. “We aren’t, are we?”

She hated seeing the look of pain that drew across his face, but she would not let it distract her.

“Sebastian. Logdok needs our help. I need you to -“

“No.” All of a sudden ice penetrated his voice again.

“Sebastian- “ she tried again, but to no avail.

“You know I’ll give you whatever you want Devlin, but not that. I cannot sit aside while goblins threaten another person I -“ He caught himself suddenly as Theo eyed him curiously. He cleared his throat.

“I will not do it. I won’t help you with this.” He tried again, resolute in his words despite the whisky.

“Seb, Logodk can help us against Rangrok! You’re being so unreasonable -“

His gaze burned into her.

“Oh for Merlin’s sake! If you want to lay your bets with him, Theo, go right ahead, but I won’t stand here and watch you make mistakes in where you place your loyalty.”

She froze at the resentment in his tone. He really wouldn’t help her? After she had agreed to go to the Keeper’s for Anne? After all his mixed signals and the way he had toyed with her emotions tonight?

“Perhaps you’re right. Perhaps I am making mistakes in my loyalty.” She gritted out.

At her tone, Sebastian realized his mistake as his face turned ashen.

“Thee…I didn’t mean…”

She didn’t care what he meant. She spun on her heel as she threw floo powder at the flame, disappearing into a haze of green fire. The last thing she saw before crashing into her common room was the look of absolute despair painted all over Sebastian’s face.

She would go see Logdok. And she would go tonight.

She was panting as she ran and ran.

The collapse of the mines.

The flash of green.

Logdok was dead. Rangrok had attacked them both. Her mind whirled as it caught up to what had just happened. She had barely made it out in time…

Her body stumbled out of the collapsing mine, sprinting until the adrenaline began to wear off and reality wore in. Rangrok had gotten her with his Diffindo cast. Undeniable red wetness seeped into her hand from her side.

She whimpered at the sight of it. She had forgotten what her own blood looked like.

Her mind flashed back to her parents, to the accident.

It was why she dueled so hard to win. At the sight of blood..her own blood…at the sight of it she couldn’t…

Her vision started going hazy as her body crumpled at the water’s edge.

She focused on breaths, she just needed a minute - she would be fine.

“Anddd wot do we ‘ave here?”

Shit.

More loyalists. As if she hadn’t defeated 8 of them and a bloody troll before she had gone in there.

Now was not the time for them to find her like this.

She scampered to get back up, wincing as the pain in her side sharpened, her cloak had become heavier with her blood.

12 more of them came out of the edges of the encampment, likely tipped off by Rangrok that she had attempted to escape. They looked at her like hunters eyeing a wounded deer.

But she would be no one’s kill today.

She stumbled to run as fast as her body would allow, casting every damage spell she could muster…but the effort to hold her wand arm up was getting harder and harder and her ancient magic was near non existent after her use of it against the armored trolls.

She was moving too slow.

Confringo!

The spell got her on her other side, knocking her down. She battled to get back up as they neared, but she couldn’t. She was starting to bob in and out of consciousness.

Then a sound brought her back. A roar of fury.

And explosions. Bodies flying.

Flashes of green and red and startled cries from the loyalists. She tried not to think what those flashes of green might have been.

She looked up to see the back of a figure with death raining down all around him.

Sebastian.

His body shook with the effort as he landed the final blow. He had gotten them single handedly. Every last one.

“Bloody Goblins..” She heard him curse as he spotted her, his footsteps pounded on the ground as he rushed over to where she lay.

She was vaguely conscious of the way his hands gingerly cusped the back of her neck, bringing her against him and moving the auburn hair clinging to her face, his soft but firm voice just barely shaking at the sight of her so injured.

“Mind your head. I’ve got you. Settle down.”

Her body calmed slightly at his words, but she still winced and struggled against him as her hand dropped from her side to reveal the wound. She heard his sharp inhale of breath. Another curse as he realized the seriousness of it.

“No..no..no no.”

He quickly shifted her, working swiftly to remove her cloak, undoing the corset and shifting up her shirt so he could inspect the wound. She heard him hiss.

“I need to tend to this now, Devlin. This may sting. Just be still. It’s going to be okay.”

He leaned her against his chest to steady her and keep her from moving, with one arm pressing her to him as his wand pressed against her bare and torn stomach.

Sharp heat filled her abdomen. She cried out.

“I know I know….I’m sorry Thee. I’m so sorry.” His voice was breaking. “It’s going to be okay it’s almost done.”

Her body started to shake as the heat from whatever spell he was casting bore into. Into her bones. Into her soul.

The convulsions were too much. She collapsed against him as he continued to work, fading into black and the last thing she could feel was a desperate kiss against her forehead, and his voice hastily repeating:

Stay with me. Please. I love you. Please. I love you. I love you..

Hours later, her eyes slowly opened in confusion. How was she not dead?

Then her mind flashed to Sebastian. He had found her somehow. He had saved her.

She was back in the Undercroft. Sebastian must have…carried her back her somehow. Underneath her, there was a makeshift bed of pillows and her bloodied shirt had been completely removed. She looked down to her side, the only evidence of her almost fatal wound was three small scars, like claw marks, braised upon her side.

He had healed her somehow.

Her head turned at the sound of a relieved breath exhaling.

She must have been dreaming, because the first thing she saw was Sebastian Sallow with her blood all over him, tear marks streaked down the blood and dirt encasing his face from it all. Had he been watching her sleep?

“Sebastian?”

“Oh thank god. You scared the hell out of me.” He rushed over to her, pulling her into his chest. She felt him tremble.

“I should’ve come with you, Thee. I’m so sorry. I’m so so sorry.”

Sebastian had been cursing himself since he brought her back here. Watching every rise and fall of her chest. Seeing her…so broken. It unlocked an entirely new level of fear inside of him. That she would never open those turbulent grey eyes again. That she would never hear his name on those lips. He would never forgive himself for almost loosing her. For almost allowing their last conversation to the one he had made as a drunken fool to her in the pub.

“How…?” Her weak voice questioned.

“A..a healing charm.” He sheepishly rubbed the back of his neck.

At her perplexed look, he responded simply, “You know I read a lot. Not all the spells I’ve studied are so unforgivable, Devlin. Magic is a tool. It’s not good or bad. What would’ve been bad ….is what could’ve happened to you because of my foolishness.”

His voice hollowed out a the prospect of it before continuing.

“I - I realized how much of a daft fool I had been in Hogshead. I went to find you after I took a sobering potion…and I found Ominis instead. He told me you had already gone. I…I was so afraid I was going to get there too late.”

He swallowed, the fear palpable in his words. She was starting to understand, she thought, as he slowly confirmed what she was thinking.

“All my life…all my life I’ve only had Anne… And now I have you. And that terrifies me.”

The truth came out, and he would not meet her gaze.

“I’m not going anywhere Sebastian.” She said softly.

“You cannot tell me that for certain.” He smiled at her sadly.

“And I know there’s…something broken inside of me. I cannot be what you need, yet I want so badly to be…you have the power to do so much, how can I allow you to settle for less then that? But I also cannot forgive myself if I don’t tell you this.”

He looked at her seeing her in an entirely new way, knowing this was the moment before the fall.

His eyes clouded at the way her soft features regarded him and what he would do next. His girl, always one step ahead. His charge. The girl that in a few weeks became so much more than that, with every sarcastic response she threw at him, and her fearless loyalty that he had never deserved.

He took her in two steps before he enveloped her entirely, picking her up into his arms, cradling her against him as he traced a thumb across her cheek.

“I love you, Theodora Devlin. I love you. Though, I wish for your sake I didn’t.”

The admonition breathed into her as she sighed at his words.

He was such a fool. She grabbed either side of his freckled and flushed face, until he looked at her completely. Those lush lips parted in wonder as he watched her in suspense. But she wanted him to look at her fully, so he knew the truth of her words.

“You cannot make those decisions for me Sebastian. You cannot eliminate all possibilities of pain for those you care about. Life doesn’t work that way. If there’s pain it will be worth it. If there’s hardship, we will face it together. I will not let you settle for anything less than that. For less than experiencing life in it’s entirety. I want to feel each bit of it with you. I want to feel everything. Because I love you too. You damned fool.”

Something in him broke at her words.

He leaned forward, closing the space and any doubt left between them. His touch was fire burning alongside her arms, her sides. His kisses pressed against her lips, coaxing them to open against him, for him. His delicious mouth moved towards that neck he loved so, then her shoulders, her stomach. Her side. He loved it all, he wondered at the thought of it. The truth that was finally free. He loved her so damned much. And she loved him.

His eyes sharpened as he reached those scars etched upon her body. Wincing at the evidence of any destruction done to that beautiful skin. She was his. And he had allowed someone to do this to her.

“I…I’m sorry I wasn’t able to heal these more cleanly,” he sorrowfully muttered against her, looking up at her in shame.

She took her hand and placed it along his face, as he closed his eyes and leaned into her.

“I’m not.” Her gaze bore into him. “You saved me, Sebastian. We’ve saved each other. Let this be a reminder of that instead.”

She drew him back up to her gently, before kissing him harder, relishing in the exhale of breath that left him as her mouth met his.

The love they made was slow….urgent… his eyes trailed each move of her body as he pressed soft kisses along each inch of her skin.

He paid particular close attention to those small scars now running across her side, pressing his lips against them reverently, uttering her name across them like a benediction.

If he believed in nothing, he could not deny that he at least believed in her.

She was, and had been in many ways, his salvation.

He would not lose her. He could not lose her. He would do what it took to protect her.

Regardless of the cost.
