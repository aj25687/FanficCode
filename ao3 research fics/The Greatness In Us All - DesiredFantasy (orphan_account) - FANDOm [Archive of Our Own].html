The Greatness In Us All

DesiredFantasy (orphan_account)

Summary:

(Arcane: League of Legends) Viktor x (Inventor) Female Reader

❗️[On Hold Till Further Notice]❗️

[This story is cross-posted on Wattpad, also just a fair warning that not everything in this fic follows exactly everything from the show, so yeah.]

Viktor didn't expect Jayce to find another fellow inventor (that was his childhood friend) who was willing to help them with the work of Hextech. The fellow inventor was you, an inspired young woman in her mid 20's with the same dream as the two young men, advancing humanity in hopes of creating a better future. At first Viktor didn't like the sound of the idea, but Jayce kept telling him that you're essential to their research, their key to finally solve the mystery behind the powerful gemstones.

After all...you and Viktor weren't so different from one another. You two just needed time to realize that.

Chapter 1: Chapter 1: Reunited

Summary:

You and Jayce reunite again and he invites you to help him and a colleague of his with a special project. How could you say no to such an opportunity?

Chapter Text

"Thank you so much for offering your help for this project Miss Y/N." 

You rolled your eyes at him in amusement, knowing he only called you that to bother you.

"Oh Jayce of course I would. This work of yours and your colleague  sounds...quite intriguing to me, after all, worth my while during my stay here." You said, bumping Jayce's arm with yours playfully. "And could you please not call me 'Miss Y/N'? I'd be embarrassing hearing you call me that in front of your colleague for god sake."

You hear Jayce laugh as you two continued walking down the long hallways of the academy. 

"I'm not making any promises."

Ah, typical Jayce. Jayce was still the same as he was when the last time you've talked to him many years ago, at the same time of course he's changed in some ways. He's matured, a more formed personality, more confident, and looks of course. You used to be taller then him years ago, but here he was towering over you 2-3 inches tall now.

It was hard to remember how long it's been since the first time you've been met Jayce, and the last time you've seen him. It's been too long. You believe you met him through school before he moved up with his life, around 15-16 at least. After that, it was kind of like a blur as the both of you went your separate ways in life.

You didn't think Jayce still stayed in the city of Piltover once you came to visit the city again, but luckily for you, you guys bumped into each other while being out and about in the city. Jayce didn't recognize you at first till you mentioned you've heard about something big going on. Something about a revolutionary discovery. Somehow word got around about it after a while on your travels. You remembered the first thing he said once he remember who you were;

"You're definitely not the same girl I once knew, that's for sure. What happened with 'hating' science and it was 'too complicated?'"

Jayce found it hard to believe you became something you swore to stay away from. Quite coincidental he must say. He was only saying all that though cause you were 'one' of those kids, but you always treated Jayce better then anyone else and didn't bully him. You weren't that malicious. Your parents wouldn't have it if they found out or saw you do that.

Just for this moment along, you explained to him where you were all these years. You traveled and went to a few academies to gain more knowledge under your belt to become a scientist yourself. Jayce got an ego boost just hearing that, thinking he inspired you to become a scientist yourself. Yes half of it was true, but there were others reason too you couldn't share. It was personal.

You found it funny how things were all placed together. You never excepted to ever encounter Jayce again (knowing how life was), but here you were hanging and laughing again with him. You were having a good time for once. Being away from the ciaos and the strict ass scientist you've met. Yes science as a job is mean to be taken seriously, but it can also be fun in many ways. 

It got even better when Jayce hinted about this "particular "project" you remembered hearing about, but rumors where rumors, after all, but Jayce explained it wasn't just a rumor. The crazy son of a bitch thought it was a create idea to bring you on board onto the project. You thought something wasn't right in his head, but he explained that he and another colleague of his have been looking for more people for it. They both wanted to find fellow scientists with the same dream as them, making humanity better as a whole, so of course you didn't decline, after all, you had nothing else on your plate besides visiting for a couple months. Even if that plan changed you wouldn't mind living here, after all, it was the city of progress, and there were many opportunities for you here. Just your luck Jayce offered it, a friend he hasn't seen in years. He could have picked anyone else. In a way you felt privileged because if it.

What you didn't expect from the handsome scientist was him asking if you were free. You thought of the most simple answer to why he asked, probably to just go grab a coffee and hang out, so you said yes. It wasn't a lie either, there was a reason you have been walking around the city most of the time. Jayce's face lit up once he got your answer, grapping your hand and taking you to where his lab was. So technically you guys were going to hang out but in a beautiful lab. You were beyond excited, and now you guys were almost making it to the lab.  

"Hey Jayce, so about your project." You began, "You haven't explained about it fully, you care to explain about it soon?" As curious as you were, you didn't want to seem pushy or demanding, or at least you hoped you didn't sound like either one of the two.

"Oh, right, well I'll explain everything once were in the lab." Jayce explained as he pulled you along. You rolled your eyes as you let your old friend drag you like a child would.

Normally you'd be pissed about getting pulled around, but over the years you've grown more patient to foolery just as this. Just as you were going to pull your arm from his grip, Jayce let you go when he walked over to the door. You see him knock on the door in a strange matter, not just the simple 'knock knock knock'. Must have been some sort of code to indicate it was Jayce and not someone else, smart, you thought as you kept your distance. From where you were standing, you heard a muffled "come in" from the other side of the door. That must be his collogue from the other side, not going to lie, you were excited to meet him. He sounded brilliant by what Jayce told you a little bit so far. Before Jayce opened the rather large door, you adjusted your clothing before following his lead. As the door opened more, you were left in awe from what you saw.

Hours of work, equations, tech, books, and papers were scattered along the room, including something that seemed...oddly familiar to you. A blue gem shining brightly in a machine that a tall, slim man was fiddling with. You tried to remember where you would have seen such a gem before, but Jayce pulled you into a side hug abruptly. You rolled your eyes at this, a light blush forming on your cheeks and ears.

"Viktor buddy, I brought someone to help us out!" Jayce explained, walking the both of you closer to the mysterious yet intriguing man.

The man didn't seem amused however, seeming too focused on the gem stone in front of him. All he had to responded under his breathe with was a simple "Lovely" before tinkering on the gadget some more. He didn't even turn around to look at the both of you. Kind of rude, but...was he being sarcastic? Sassy even? Cause it sure sounded like it to you. At least it seemed like he wasn't another stuck up scientists like the many you've encountered in your years of study. You were proud of Jayce for finding someone that wasn't a dick, thank the gods.

Jayce let go of you and walked over to the busy man, putting a hand on his shoulder. "Just stop for a minute and let me properly introduce you to Y/N here. She's an old friend of mine."

The man named Viktor let out a sigh, putting his tools down. "Really now?" As Viktor turned around in his seat, he took his safely goggles off as his gaze land on you.

Your gaze went from looking around to his glistening yellow, golden eyes. You felt like you froze in place just by his gaze alone. His eyes were quite captivating, but you managed to pry your eyes from his to examine the rest of what he looked like. Somewhat neatly-combed brown hair, his fascial structure was sharp looking, but his face looked gentle to you. You noticed he had moles on his face, specifically near his lip and cheek bone... Ok now you were looking for way long for comfort. You only just met this man, but here you were eye boggling him, but you couldn't help yourself. He was quite an attractive man, after all, but looks wasn't what you focused on when it came to men. A man of smarts and looks was hard to find, but here was a prime example of that case (aside from Jayce, but you only saw him as a friend.) You honestly felt flustered just standing there, quite shocked even, but you'd just have to see what Viktor was like before thinking anything else. 

Once you got out of the childish trance, you let out a chuckle to sooth the awkward tension, walking forward. You lifted your arm and presented your hand to him. "L-Like Jayce here said, I'm Y/N, Y/N F/N." You said as you tried your best to cover your shyness.

You see a faint smile on the man's lips, reaching over to grab what looked like a cane. As Jayce stepped back, Viktor sat himself up, applying some of his weight onto the cane. As he stood up, your eyes follow his movement, his hand moving to yours to shake it.

"Viktor."

As he spoke, you noticed he sounded less annoyed, but intrigued by your presence, but wow that's not what you excepted him to sound like once he opened his mouth. He said his name like butter as it rolled off his tongue in a soft spoken, Russian accent. You had to admit, it was cute. You wouldn't mind him ranting or talking hours about anything. You could see now that even if he was slightly slouching, he was taller then you as well as he shook your hand. His grip was firm, yet his hand was cold like yours, just the way you liked it. He sounded confidence, which was fascinating. Viktor didn't just have the look or the smarts, but the voice as well. As you pulled your hand away from his, if you had to be honest with yourself, being around a man like Viktor would cause you to be a tad distracted. He seemed way too interesting to just ignore.

Jayce noticed something between the two of you as you first looked at each other and shook hands, but he just couldn't put his finger on it yet as he put an arm around your guys shoulders, grinning ear to ear. "Now that's done, come Y/N let me show you some of the progress we've made so far."

You feel Jayce move as he put a hand on your back, pushing you along to the table in front of you as he began to explain the whole project to you. You totally forgot about this bit. Dealing with Jayce's lack of personal space bothered you, but it quickly faded away as you started taking info in that Jayce was showing and explaining to you. Trying to understand everything he was saying was starting to make sense as you picked up some paper and examples of porotypes and theories, asking questions along the way. You seemed way out it cause you didn't notice Viktor was beside you, but not that close. You saw him waving some particular notes in your field of vision. You turn to him, seeming sarcastic again as he grinned at you.

"I think you'll need to look at these notes first in particular to catch up on what Jayce and I are working on currently. Shouldn't take you too long." Viktor explained as you grabbed the notes from his hand. Before you could thank him, he started walking back to where he was previously, your eyes following him on their own.

From that you could feel another set of eyes on you, and you knew who. You let out a sigh as you turned your gaze onto Jayce's. By the look in his eyes and facial expression, he was about to say something stupid in the next couple seconds. You see his sharp eyebrows raise quickly, a teasing grin rising on his face before returning to examine more notes. Your eyebrows scrunch up seeing that. You knew he was doing that just to get to you, but this time something felt different. You couldn't put your finger on it. Before Jayce opened his mouth, you spoke before he could in a whisper;

"Shut it Tallis. Don't even think about it."

Jayce blinked, leaning closer to you in a whisper. "Think about what? Why are you whispering?"

Oh that bitch.

You kept your silence following forward, your focus going to read the notes that Viktor handed to you previously. You weren't going to let Jayce have his way of teasing you again like he's done years ago. Even though he was considered a grown ass man now, you still saw him as a dork, as silly as that sounded calling him that now. Jayce was only having fun picking at you again though as a joke, but what he could see from your expression you had a glitter in your eye. You seemed rather focused yet peaceful. Jayce had a feeling in the next while things are going to sky rocket, seeing that you had the same enthusiasm as him and Viktor.

Even with that said though, Viktor had his judgements about you. Not to say he didn't think you were intelligent for this, but Viktor was concerned that you'd get in the way of their research, or be proved not to be useful. Besides, he wasn't good with new people, or trusting a project just as this to be shared with someone that just seemed like to appear out of nowhere. What if you leaked all this to the outside word? Jayce knew what he was doing though, so Viktor was going to trust him bringing someone else into this, but he'd make sure to keep a watchful eye on you.

But from what he's seen so far, you seemed...reasonable.
