There are brief moments where he wakes, delirious and disoriented, then slips back under. Throughout this, Narcissa has been a constant comforting presence. Despite his past animosities with Draco and Lucius, she’d never left his side, holding his hand through the worst of the pain.

Draco, too, had surprised Harry with his unwavering support. They had never been particularly close before Harry fell ill, but Draco has taken charge of the hospital room, barking orders at the healers and ensuring that Harry's pain is kept at bay which mainly involved keeping him under. 

One morning, as the first rays of artificial sunlight filters through the curtains, Harry finally begins to regain some semblance of actual consciousness. Eyelids heavy, reluctant to part, he blinks several times, struggling to focus on his surroundings. Weakened, as though every ounce of strength has been drained from his body, he turns towards Narcissa, still sitting at his bedside, her eyes red-rimmed from exhaustion.

Shifting slightly, he tries to turn but something is wrapped tightly around his limbs holding him still. Before he can puzzle through whether there’s actually something holding him or he’s just that sick still, the soft rustling of his sheets draws Narcissa's attention. 

"Harry!" she exclaims softly, a mixture of relief and joy in her voice. Gently squeezing his hand, her fingers cool against his feverish skin, she asks "How are you feeling? Any pain?”

Harry manages a weak smile, through enormous effort, "Like I've been hit by a Bludger," he croaks, parched throat barely managing a rasp.

Narcissa brushes a strand of hair from his forehead, "You've been through a great deal, but it’s going to be all right now."

Draco, engrossed in conversation with the healers, remains oblivious to Harry's awakening. Listening in, Harry can hear him quietly discussing treatment plans and monitoring protocols, his usually composed demeanor showing signs of stress and insomnia when the door swishes open aggressively.

“How heartwarming. The Malfoys playing nursemaid,” a nasally voice sneers, tone curt and disrespectful as the medi wizard strides over standing toe to toe with Draco.

“Tread carefully. I’ll have your license revoked,” Draco sneers, spine utterly straight. The other attendings shuffle awkwardly but don't intervene.

“Who are you going to get to do that? You gonna call daddy? Oh no, that's right, he’s on his way to Azkaban where the rest of you belong,“ he taunts, “The only reason you two are allowed in here is because Heir Potter, in his delusional state, signed you off as his medical proxy.”

Draco's jaw clenches, silver eyes narrowing dangerously. He’s beyond furious, but also keenly aware that he needs to tread carefully. The wizarding world's perception of the Malfoys is a delicate matter at the moment, and he’s loathe to say anything that could jeopardize Lucius' upcoming trial before it even begins.

"Your opinions about my family are irrelevant," Draco retorts, voice ice-cold, "This is a hospital room, and you will show some respect befitting your profession or I will be reporting you.”

The medi wizard snickers, seemingly unafraid, "Respect? Respect for the Malfoys? That's rich- like you used to be. A Malfoy’s words don't hold the same weight anymore.”

Before Draco can respond, Harry decides it's time to intervene. His voice, weakened from his ordeal, carries a firmness that surprises both Draco and the intruder, “Enough."

The media wizard whips to look at Harry, his sneer faltering as an oppressive weight begins settling throughout the room. Harry may be weakened, but his glare sends shivers down the interloper’s spine.

"Heir Potter-?” the man sputters, taking a step back, “We didn't expect you to wake up this soon!”

“ Obviously .” Glowering at the man he strains against the straps holding him but it just leaves him feeling drained. "Draco and his family are here as my medical proxies because I trust them. They saved my life by bringing me here and I won’t have you belittling my benefactors. Leave now, before I decide to press charges for harassment. My word happens to carry a lot of weight ,” Harry grits.

Taking a step back, unnerved by Harry's unexpected defense of the Malfoys the medi wizard reluctantly concedes.

“My apologies if what I said upset you, I was only concerned that these uncouth individuals were taking advantage of your vulnerability,” he mutters, casting one last venomous look at Draco before retreating hastily from the room.

Draco exhales a sigh of relief, his shoulders relaxing as the intruder leaves. He turns to Harry, his expression a mix of gratitude and concern. "Thank you, Harry."

“Anytime,” Harry easily offers in light of the extensive care he’s received. Turning to the remaining attendees, he demands, shaking a cuffed wrist, “A little help here? I’m awake now so I assume these aren't needed anymore . ”

“I’m sorry Heir Potter but I don't have the authority to remove these. The head of our department has to sign off on that,” one young man informs him, fidgeting with his clipboard. Swishing his wand, a ringing sound chimes from the hall as he explains, “You exhibited some rather alarming behaviors while we were stabilizing you that resulted in injuries to your attendees.”

“You bit several of them and actually managed to eat a chunk of one of the nurses,” Draco deadpans, green pallor suggesting he isn't joking. 

Cogs turning Harry vaguely remembers the delicious taste of what he thought was pie sliding down his throat. Gagging he shoves his face against the pillow and groans with dismay.

“Yeah, pretty fucked up,” Draco agrees, his Mother barely managing a disapproving glint in his direction.

“It’s not so surprising. Your temperature rose all the way to forty-four degrees celsius. A muggle would have died long before then, so some erratic behavior is to be expected,” the mediwizard states, trying to be reassuring but his face is pale and sweat dots his temples as he recalls the incident.

Lifting his head from the pillow Harry stares disbelievingly at the man. Lips parting, he goes to ask how that's possible when the door slides open.

Several new people come rushing into the room.

"Heir Potter," calls the ginger haired elder leading the newest group of attendees as she steps towards him. Wand swishing over him, a page unrolls midair and a quick quote quill begins rapidly jotting down notes, "Dr. Pedra, a pleasure to make your acquaintance. We didn't expect you to get up so soon, but I'm happy to see such improvement." Wand swishing, the quill pauses and the scroll rolls up.

Gently, she asks, "There is much to go over, but before we begin, how are you feeling this evening?"

"Better than I've felt these last few weeks, still sore," Harry admits, eyeing the Doctor with slightly less apprehension. "This obviously wasn't some common cold I'm guessing?" he jokes.

"You're right, you weren't sick, just overwhelmed. You've had some very rapid magical growth and it's put a huge strain on your body. Your immune system is actually fantastic," she praises, "Your magic appears to be supplementing your immune and healing factors, boosting them to a degree. I doubt you've been ill too many times in your life."

"Magical growth…?" Harry asks, confused, "Why would my magic growing have done this? I thought I already reached the age of magical majority."

The Malfoy's stiffen and scowl, meanwhile the attendants shuffle awkwardly looking uncomfortable. Dr. Pedra gently clasps his shoulder as if trying to comfort him. 

Dread pools in his gut.

"What you are going through is unprecedented, and a travesty Heir Potter," the Dr. begins, "You had a crippling magical block on your core. From our scans we've assumed that it broke when you were hit with a killing curse, and for the first time in your entire life your core was finally given room to flourish -but that's why your body began to suffer."

Pausing, she pokes at the veins in his arm and says, "Anatomically witches and wizards are very similar to muggles besides some slight deviances. Very close to our heart we hold our magical core, which is connected to channels that follow the same paths as veins and arteries. As magical children age, the core begins to produce more and more magic, which slowly expands those magical channels throughout the body to accommodate the increase."

"These channels only widen so much, usually that's sufficient to hold all the magic our cores can output," she halts once more and reveals, "You Heir Potter are an exception. In all of history there have only been a handful of individuals that have had such a surplus of magic that it overflowed into their cardiovascular system as well."

Expression solemn, she informs, "This normally wouldn't have been a problem, but neither your magical channel's or veins have had any sufficient growth since you were a toddler. Your core flooded your body, bursting the channel's, putting strain on your heart and other organs. Your magic tried to counter the damage, which is the only reason you've survived, because as I mentioned earlier, it's boosting your healing factor. Your body is trying to heal itself at the same rate the damage is occurring but your core keeps growing."

She smiles gently, "Thankfully your body is a fighter. We're doing our best to assist it by restraining the growth to a more manageable rate. Your restraints are made to slow the growth so it doesn't surpass your healing factor. As long as you remain calm we can help maintain the slow growth until your core fully settles and your body has adapted to the increased output. "

"It isn't an exaggeration when I say magic flows through your very veins and is continuing to grow. We know individuals such as yourself to have 'King's blood' because when you bleed, due to the excess output, it will flow out a golden color, as we witnessed on your arrival," she rushes, trying to remain professional yet clearly enthused.

"A magical block?" He utters, voice devoid of expression.

"Yes," she answers soberly, excitement draining as quickly as it arrived, "It was placed on you when you were very young. If your magic wasn't healing you at the same rate it was destroying you, -if you hadn't arrived in time for us to dampen your magic? …You would have imploded, potentially destroying the surrounding area."

"Who placed the block on me?" He grits, hands clenching as he braces for the knowledge. Only a handful of people were twisted enough to have done this to him. He has a sick feeling that he knows exactly which of them it was.

"...Albus Dumbledore's magical signature was found at the source," she quietly reveals, confirming his suspicions. Pained, she sighs, "I can't believe he'd do such an atrocious thing."

" I can, he was a bloody lunatic ," Draco sneers, clearly already in the know.

" That man never valued the lives of others, only his own vision. It's why in spite of his exaggerated accomplishments, the Malfoys never joined him," Narcissa sneers. Turning to Harry, she soothes, "While he may have taken your past, he cannot touch your future."

Snapping her fingers, Dr. Pedra takes a handful of papers from her aide and hands them to Draco, trying to move forward, "We've come up with a care regimen for you to follow once you're discharged which might take a few more weeks, we'll have to watch and see-."

"Merlin's saggy-!" Draco balks wide eyed, raking through the pages with increasing horror.

"Draco!" Narcissa hisses, cutting him off. Silently she holds her hand out insistently, and after a moment, a shaken Draco hands her the papers. Face bloodless she utters, "Oh…"

"Most of the damage is treatable, your prognosis is excellent, and your healing factor will go a long way in the coming months as you transition," Dr. Pedra insists, looking to Narcissa she reassures, "There's only one thing we can't treat, but it's still manageable- he just needs to avoid future killing curses."

"That's not why I-! I mean yeah this is all bloody awful, but -but what do you mean by putting that name as his paternal-? How is that possible?!" Draco gawks.

"That horrible old man," Narcissa sneers as she reads the name. Livid, the paper crumples in her grasp. "Harry. I'm so sorry. None of us knew. I'm certain your father had no idea…"

"What-?"

Overwhelmed, Harry looks as she shows him the first page and freezes as he reads : 

 

Relatives:

Paternal:

Father- Severus Snape

Grandfather- Tobias Snape

Grandmother- Eileen Snape nee Prince

Maternal:

Mother: Lily Potter nee Evans nee Peverell

Grandfather- Auberon Peverell

Grandmother- 

 

"Why?" He croaks, drained. Years of painful interactions, insults, and discrimination… What was it all for? Why would anyone make him believe James Potter was his father? Why would someone not want him to know that Severus Snape was his real father? Who-?

Dumbledore .

Grieved, Harry lifts his hands to cover his face, when the restraints yank them to a halt. Defeated, his arms fall back to the bed and he closes his weeping eyes.

All this time. He could have had a father, a real family, all this time , but once again Dumbledore has taken that from him. He never had a chance.

Sure, he and Snape never had the most pleasant of interactions, but maybe they could have. Maybe they could have had an actual bond, gotten the chance to know one another, helped one another…

Maybe they both could have lived. Now Snape's dead and he'll never know what could have been. 

A strong pair of arms wrap around his shoulders.

Pressing up into the warmth, he weeps, grieving this latest loss. He is so sick of losing the people he cares about, and he hadn't even had a chance to care this time.

He's devastated.

Throat burning, he stifles his sobs and demands coldly, "Well go on then. There's more isn’t there?" The comforting arms stiffen and slowly withdraw, revealing a discomfited Draco.

"Can't be any worse than finding out you have a dad but you missed the chance to know him by two bloody weeks ," he sneers.

Draco sighs, pulling out a handkerchief, "I wouldn't be too sure… it's more of your usual incorrigible brand of luck."

Quietly, Narcissa holds out the next page for him to see while Draco pats at Harry's face.

Taking a deep breath, he dares to look:

 

Underlying injuries, indications, and contraindications:

Basilisk venom, status: active, untreatable

Prolonged exposure to Basilisk venom neutralized by Phoenix tears resulting in immunity to lesser venoms. Cross check all patient potions before use to avoid negative  reactions.

Phoenix tears, status: active, untreatable

Prolonged exposure to Phoenix tears resulting in increased purifying qualities to bodily fluids and increased resistance to poisons and potions. Suspected increase in longevity. Cross check all patient potions before use to avoid negative reactions.

Strain on heart, status: active, treatable

Burst veins and artery, status: active, treatable

Recurring bleeding in lungs, status: active, treatable

Magical core, status: active, expanding rapidly, manageable

Horcrux, status: expunged, soul untreatable

Horcrux removal via killing curse caused damage to the bearer's soul.

 

Harry pauses, heart skipping a beat as he reads the crisply written words of salvation.

There is no Horcrux. He didn't fail after all…

Eyes watering anew with bitter relief and cautious joy he continues perusing the information:

 

Soul damage, status: active, untreatable

Exposure to multiple killing curses and horcrux removal leading to significant damage to the soul. Miraculous unidentifiable signature used to patch pieces of the soul. Source unknown.

Curses:  

Intelligence block

Vision impairment

Loyalty charm

Height impairment

Facial alterations 

Core Restriction

Magical signature: Albus Dumbledore

Status: broken/fading due to the death of victim Heir Potter

 

“He really put blocks on me. Knowing how bad it would be. Knowing that if they broke I would die, thus ensuring that I would die,” Harry grits, blood boiling.

All this time, he wanted Harry dead, and this was his failsafe. He was supposed to die even if he won. That's what Dumbledore wanted?! 

In spite of the strong magic dampening cuffs surrounding his wrists, the room begins to give a metallic groan, bottles and utensils falling to the ground with a crash. 

"And what the hell does this mean? Loyalty charms? Intelligence blocks? He even changed my Merlin forsaken face?!" Harry roars. One of the bed legs shrieks as it crumples, lurching precariously on its now three legs. One of the aides swiftly casts a levitation charm, keeping the bed upright.

" WHY ?! What the fuck did I ever do to that bastard?! ” He roars, yanking at his straps. 

“Heir Potter please ! You can't over exert yourself! The cuffs can only dampen so much. We barely managed the right ratio of dampening to allow minimal damage to your body. If you can't calm down we will have to put you back under before your magic destroys the cuffs. This will be a huge setback. You might be back to square one or worse !” Dr. Pedra pleads, attempting to use logic with him though she’s panicking. 

Another bed leg begins to groan in protest, wands point at Harry in preparation.

Hastily, Draco grabs his shoulders and lectures, “Yeah, apparently Dumbledore wanted you dead - So what ? The most powerful wizards hunted you year after year , cursed you, crippled you, tried to kill you , and yet who’s sitting here now? Voldemort? Dumbledore? They're all dead , but not you Harry."

"This? All this is just their leftover bullshit! You won! Now act like it,” Draco insists, shaking him.

One by one items stop trembling and groaning as the blondes' sincerity settles warmly in his chest.

“You should have been in hufflepuff, that was downright cheery,” Harry sniffs, a tiny upward twitch to his lips. 

Draco sneers, ignoring him to peer over his mothers shoulder at the papers she's been crumpling in distress. Something must catch his eyes because he glares at the healers. Plucking a page out he waves it at them and demands, “What else did you find? This many unnecessary blood panels, I'm sure you found something of note. Did you suspect another root cause? Or was this just to satisfy some sick curiosity over the Savior's blood?”

Glancing at Harry, Dr. Pedra seems somewhat apologetic, but she doesn't deny the accusations.

“As I explained earlier, Heir Potter's core is enormous. On a scale from one to Merlin he’s on par and growing . That kind of power is unheard of, since even before the goblin wars. Recently both muggleborn and purebloods have seen declines in power, occasionally we get a few outliers, but this kind of ability is far beyond the scope of normal. It's almost inhuman .”

Eyes narrowed, Narcissa demands, “You think there is a creature inheritance?”

“We tested for that, looked into bloodlines to be sure but no, it isn't - at least not any creature we’ve ever come across. We did find that the source is from his mother's line. Whatever she was, Lily Potter was never just a muggleborn.”

“So what was she?” Harry grits, tired of circling around the answer.

“I don't know how, because it's historically and scientifically impossible based on our knowledge of bloodlines, but Lily Potter appears to have been a direct descendant of the golden line,” she breathes, astounded and amazed by the discovery.

Narcissa gapes and Draco whips to stare at Harry in disbelief.

“But he doesn't- his blood is red! The bloody moron gets injured all the time and I've never once seen-!” Draco balks.

“You wouldn't have, because of the core block. His magic never got the chance to grow enough to produce the characteristic golden blood,” the doctor solemnly informs them. “It probably won't be pure gold for a while yet. Currently it's more of a bright orange since his core is still expanding and healing, but once it’s finished I'm certain it’ll be gleaming gold.”

“Dumbledore is a monster,” Narcissa frowns, hands curling into fists, “Does that mean he knew about Lily? Did he block her core as well?! Merlin, it's just all so horrible. I'm so sorry Harry…” Gently she moves to hold his hand but Harry is having a hard time understanding.

“What's a 'golden line'?” he groans, wishing his hands were free so he could pinch his nose. Which is when he notices; where are his glasses? How is he seeing everyone so clearly? Blinking, astonished, he goes to ask if they managed to fix his eyes when Dr. Pedra continues.

“You are the direct descendant to the royal line. The origin line of the magical world. The existing bloodlines now are so far from the source that not one wizard besides yourself could hope to claim their blood is royal. It would be a joke if any other tried, yet here you are. A prime candidate for the throne after centuries of magical power decline,” she enthuses, eyes gleaming. 

“We learn about all the rarest blood types in mediwizard training but yours was purely theoretical and the only information available is on fully developed gold blooded individuals. To be able to bear witness to an actual historical transition in such a short time span… You're the most groundbreaking patient study to date,” she finishes, clearly struggling to withhold her glee.

Hair fluffing indignantly, Draco towers protectively over Harry’s bewildered form. “A groundbreaking study, hm? Are you planning to publish his -as you stated, unique results? What about patient confidentiality? I thought there were protocols in place to prevent such a breech, especially for such a high profile figure. One who would become even more high profile should your findings be published!”

Paling, Dr. Pedra fidgets under his glare, "We- the hospital will do our best to prevent leaks but the Minister was very persistent in his need for reassurance of Heir Potter’s well-being and there are laws requiring full disclosure and documentation on any rightful Heirs to the magical throne! Preparations have to be made and the governing Lord's must be notified of the imminent change in ruling. A study being published won't change the fact that the public must be informed soon as well," She apologetically explains.

“I don't understand. My blood is changing colors and somehow that means I'm royalty?” Harry croaks, overwhelmed.

“Wizarding Britain is an Oligarchy at the moment, ruled by purebloods and a few well connected individuals who vote in laws, but only because no eligible heir was ever in place to inherit the throne. Until six days ago when you came to this hospital,” Dr. Pedra explains. Sincerely bowing, she finishes, “My liege.”

A moment later the other nurses nervously bow as well.

"...I need you all to leave. Get out, " Harry snaps. Closing his eyes against the cacophony in his brain, he hopes that this has all been a nightmare, and when he opens his eyes he'll have just had some wizard's cold.

Eyes reluctantly opening a few -minutes? -hours? later, he sees only the Malfoy's are still in the room and they look incredibly solemn yet resolved.

Not a nightmare then.

"Harry, you've been through many extremes in your life, and I know you weren't expecting anything like this, I don't think anyone could have, but we will do our best to support you through this trifling time," Narcissa swears.

"Besides Dumbledore you mean. He probably knew and did all this so I wouldn't live to know I was golden-whatever . Wish he was here so I could tell him I never wanted a ruddy throne, so he can take it and shove it up his-" Harry snaps.

"You're wrong Potter. That's how Dumbledore wanted you to feel. That's how all those curses wearing off have convinced you to feel, but you haven't been free of his control until now , so how can you possibly know that you don't want the throne? Don't you think it'd be great to take back all the things he stole from you? Besides, that ruddy physician already spilled to the Ministry. Kneazles out of the bag Prince Potter," Draco huffs with a mocking bow.

“So what am I supposed to do?” Harry utters, feeling cold and drained, “How can I figure out what I want if I was under some curse my entire life? How will I know what’s actually me?”

“You'll have to pay attention to yourself. You have to watch what you're doing and see if it's an empty gesture or something you actually want to do. It won't be easy but eventually you will be able to dig out whatever is actually you under all this Gryffindor trim,” Draco offers, poking him in the chest.

“I was supposed to be in Slytherin,” Harry reminds him, trying to joke, but it falls flat because now he’ll never know if that was where he was meant to be all along. He’s a stranger to himself.

“...” Draco frowns, glaring off to the side, then whips to Harry and insists, “You are a Slytherin. Hell, you were the sneakiest of us, you snuck right on into the house of lions and became the Savior of the Wizarding World. That was pretty damn ambitious of you Potter.”

Eyes moist, Harry chuckles and asks, “How’d a Hufflepuff like you get in Slytherin then?”

“Because I wanted to be loyal to you but some idiot didn't shake my hand. Obviously ,” Draco deadpans, lip curling with disdain. 

“Stop, I’ll get cavities,” Harry groans, turning to smush his face into the pillow to muffle his snickering. Peering at the blonde he inquires, “Did they say when I can go home?”

“Dr. Pedra said they wanted you here for observation no less than two weeks to monitor you until your magical growth becomes more consistent, “Narcissa informs him, “She said right now there are random spikes of growth that they have to be present to manage, but eventually it should stabilize so they can provide you with the correct amount of magical restraints throughout your growth, which they believe will cease after a few months and settle into your new normal. Then there will be supervised sessions to reestablish your control.”

Two weeks…

Groaning with dismay he buries his face back into the comfortable down, resigning himself to an insufferable hospital stay. 

Draco half-mockingly pats his head.
