Work Text:

Sitting on the bank of the river, Katara watched the sun slip behind the mountains as night settled over the land.  

 

They had escaped from the pirates — and Zuko — by the skin of their teeth, a predicament they only ended up in thanks to her own insecurity and that stupid scroll.

 

Although Aang and Sokka had easily forgiven her, Katara still felt guilty. She had endangered them all, and she never wanted to put any of them in that position ever again.

 

With his light-footed steps, Katara didn’t even notice that Aang had appeared beside her until he placed the waterbending scroll in her lap. Immediately, Katara tried to hand it back, but Aang gently pushed it away with a small shake of his head.

 

“I can’t figure out a lot of these moves,” he said, gesturing vaguely to the rolled parchment. “Will you work on them with me?” He looked at her with hopeful eagerness, his bright gray eyes seeming to glow orange in the dwindling sunlight. 

 

Katara turned the scroll over in her hands, running her index finger over the wax seal imprinted with the delicate swirls of the Water Tribe emblem. She sighed. 

 

“I don’t know, Aang. You saw how I acted before. I hated that part of me. So immature and competitive. It got us in serious trouble. You could have gotten hurt, or worse. I don’t know what I would have done with myself if that had happened. Besides, I don’t think you need my help much, anyway.” 

 

She said it without a trace of animosity or bitterness because it was the truth. Aang was the Avatar, after all. It was only natural that he would be able to pick up bending skills at a faster rate than most. 

 

“That’s not true. I do need your help, Katara,” Aang insisted. He sat down next to her and leaned in. “Please? I-I really like practicing with you.”

 

Katara looked at him and felt a sudden warmth rise in her cheeks. Their faces were just inches apart now and Aang was gazing at her, eyes wide and shiny, playfully pleading. His bottom lip jutted out then in a sweet pout, and Katara couldn’t help but smile. How could she refuse him?

 

Nodding, Katara got to her feet and helped him up. “Okay. Let's get started."
