Work Text:

“Since when did you and Ginny break up?” Ron barges into Harry’s room.

“Good morning to you too,” Harry sighs.

After the Battle, Harry and Ron were fully prepared to drop out of school. They thought it would be pointless to even finish, considering the amount of stress they went through. But after months of Hermione’s nagging, they finally caved.

“I know you two can AT LEAST graduate from Hogwarts! It’s only another year plus we can worry about school for once instead of Voldemort trying to kill us!”

Although Ron and Harry hated to admit it at the time, they both saw Hermione’s point and decided to go back. Fortunately with Professor McGonagall as the new Headmaster, she thought it would be fair for the three of them to at least finish their last year.

So, everyone in their class was given an option to finish their last year to graduate. And it seemed like everyone wanted to do just that. That included being roommates with Neville, Seamus, and Dean still. Just like old times.

“Woah, since when did that happened?” Seamus said. “I thought you two were ready to get married.”

He’s playing cards with Dean, both sitting on Seamus’ bed. Dean was equally as confused, given that he thought the same. The whole school knew about Harry and Ginny, especially since they decided to start dating.

Harry shrugs, “She and I agreed it would be best. But how did you figure it out, we just broke up yesterday.”

“A brother has ways,” Ron replies. “But that’s not the point. I’m going to talk to her and if I hear something else, I’m going to have words with you.”

Ron marches out as Neville walks in the dorm, startled by his anger.

“What’s wrong with him?”

“Harry broke up with Ginny,” Dean answers. “And apparently it was a mutual agreement.”

“Oh I’m sorry Harry. If it’s any consolation, Luna and I broke up too.”

“What?” Harry says. “I thought you two were hitting it off well!”

Neville scratches the back of his head and shrugs. “Well… let’s just say we had different opinions on some things…”

“It’s like a plague of break-ups are happening,” Seamus shakes his head. “Good thing I’m not dating anyone.”

Dean nods in agreement as Harry changes the subject, asking everyone their weekend plans. Although his break-up with Ginny was truly mutual, Harry couldn’t help but feel guilty. Ginny knew for a long time that Harry had eyes for someone else.

Someone who everyone loathes, and Harry thought he did too. But things have changed with Draco ever since he saw him in the train.

“Is that… Draco?” Hermione observes as the three sat in their compartment. It was unnecessary, but as a thank you gift, the Ministry and Hogwarts have given the three of them their own private compartment.

They are allowed to bring anyone else, but Ginny wanted to sit next with Luna and Neville felt weird sitting with them. The three of them decided to take their own compartment and told Hermione, Harry and Ron that they’re fine with sitting with the rest.

Really the three wanted them to sit next to them so it felt a little less awkward, but none of them wanted to push. Although out of everyone who could be in the train, they did not expect to see Draco.

He looks the same as did when they saw him at the Battle. He still has the same white blonde hair, yet it’s longer now. He has it in a small ponytail and his physique is a little bit skinny. He doesn’t look as sickly as before, but he still looks like he’s in recovery.

He’s also wearing regular muggle clothes, which threw everyone off. Displaying his jeans with a plain white t-shirt and light green flannel.

Draco knocks on the door then opens it, “Hello…”

“What do you want Malfoy?” Ron coldly asks. “And how the hell did you get here?”

“Ron,” Hermione scolds. “Don’t be so aggressive! But he does bring up some good questions…”

Draco sighs, “Look, I’m not here to fight. I promise. I just want to talk to Pot- I mean Harry.”

Harry looks at him then his best friends with surprise. The two also sporting the same look.

Since when did Draco say his first name?!

“Uh, sure,” Harry decides. “What’s wrong?”

“Can I-“ Draco fidgets. “Is it okay if I talk to you alone?”

Before Ron objects, Hermione says, “Ron and I can leave you two. Come on, let’s check on Ginny.”

She drags him out as Ron tries to protest, leaving the compartment while quietly bickering. Draco closes the door and sits right across from Harry.

He takes a deep breath, “Look… I know it’s insane that I’m here. Merlin, I thought I would be in Azkaban, and I know you’re thinking it too.”

Harry gives him a blank look, unsure how to respond. But before he could say anything, Draco continues.

“But by some miracle, someone decided it would be better for me to finish my schooling at Hogwarts. Who you ask, I have no clue. But I am restricted from my wand until I have proven I have changed. And that includes having a better relationship with you.”

As Draco says this, he’s looking nervously on the ground, fidgeting with this fingers. Harry can feel the awkwardness and nervousness, until Draco looks straight into his eyes.

“I’m sorry Harry. I’m sorry for everything me and my family has done. And although it doesn’t change anything, I want us to try to be friends. Please.”

A long pause of silence is followed, Harry is floored and still doesn’t know what to say. It wasn’t until Draco opens his mouth to apologize once again when Harry finally says something.

“Let’s hang out,” he blurts. “Let’s… hang out then. Once a week. Just the two of us.”

Harry’s mind is running 20 miles per hour, wondering why he said that.

Draco smiles, “That would be great! Let’s hang out on Sunday, yeah?”

Harry nods as Draco stands up and walks over to the door. He opens the door then turns around.

“Thank you…”

“For what?”

“For giving me a chance.”

Draco closes the door then walks away. Harry thought he’s hallucinating but he almost sees Draco with a little pep in his step.

Ever since that day Draco and Harry hung out every Sunday, doing whatever they want within reason. Sometimes they would get lunch somewhere in Hogsmeade, sometimes they would just wandering by the Black Lake. It didn’t matter what they did, as long as they hung out every Sunday.

At first Ron thought he lost his mind when Harry first told him and Hermione about Draco’s proposal. Hermione was also wary about this, but ultimately trust Harry that he is more than capable of handling him.

“Besides it’s not like he can hex him,” Hermione points out. “He said it himself, Draco’s wand is restricted.”

Ron grumbles with disagreements, but eventually warms up to the idea. Although the two haven’t really talked to Draco calmly and without insults, they do trust Harry.

And believe it or not, Harry looks forward to hanging out with Draco. It turns out they have a lot more in common than he thought.

“You want to become an Auror too?” Harry asks. The two are lying side by side near the Black Lake. Fall is coming in quick with the leaves turning into hues of orange and brown.

“Yes, but I highly doubt it’ll happen. I mean with what my family and I did, I can’t blame them,” Draco admits. “Although if I didn’t have the whole Deatheater thing pounded in my head, I think I would make a great Auror.”

“Well you never know. Maybe if you prove yourself more… Not that you aren’t already that is, maybe they’ll let you.”

Draco scoffs, “Right, and Ron and Hermione will forgive me and everyone will throw a party in my honor.”

Harry rolls to his side, facing Draco. He sees Draco looking at the sky with a tired face, but also with longing. Longing for more than what he did in the past.

“I know you’re being sarcastic Draco, but I really think you can do it.”

Draco turns his head over and whispers, “How can you be sure?”

“Because I’ll becoming Head and then we can both be Aurors.”

Draco laughs, “Now that would be quite the headline. ‘The Chosen One and the Fallen One are now working together as Aurors, despite everything.’ I can already see father rolling in his grave.”

Harry laughs at his comment, “No, but Draco, I’m serious. You’re doing well in all your classes anyways. I don’t see any reason you shouldn’t.”

“Ah yes, other than the fact that half of not all my family are Death Eaters.”

“Not everyone. Aren’t you distance relatives with Sirius? And look how he turned out.”

“Other than dead?” Draco murmurs.

“Draco…”

“Sorry, sorry… it’s just…” he sighs. “I don’t want more attention on me if I mess up. Everyone is already rooting for my downfall.”

“And I will be there for you every step of the way,” Harry confirms. “I won’t let you fall.”

Draco rolls over to his side, looking straight into Harry’s eyes. He tried to find the lies, the hoax, anything that indicated that Harry didn’t mean it. All he could see was adoration and determination. Traits a Gryffindor has through and through.

Draco sighs, “Oh alright. If you think so…”

Harry grins, “I know so.”

The two sit on their sides, looking at each other without realizing it. Each boy is studying each other’s faces like a painting. Like magnets they pulled each other close. Close enough to kiss.

Draco whispers, “You have specks of gold in your eyes.”

“And you have specks of sliver,” Harry whispers back. “I like it.”

“Me too… wait no, I meant I liked yours- wait- ugh, stop laughing!”

“I’m sorry! It’s just- it’s cute how you’re trying!”

“Well at least I’m trying,” Draco pouts and faces the sky.

Harry stops laughing and scoots over, his hand gently moves Draco’s chin to face him. Today Draco decides to leave his hair down instead of his usual pony tail. And although Harry won’t admit it, deep down he can feel his heart racing a bit more.

He then tucks a strain of hair on the back of his ear, and smiles.

“Well, either way, you’re adorable in my eyes.”

Draco’s eyes widen as he sits up coughing, his whole face and down to his neck turning a deep shade of cherry. Harry smirks at the boy as he sits up too, picking the nearest flower. He then gently tucks the flower in Draco’s ear, like an accessory.

“Wha-?”

Harry just shrugs, “Blue brings out your eyes more. Although I am surprised there even is a flower here since it’s the fall.”

Draco is left speechless as Harry’s grin grows wider. He then shoves Harry as he starts laughing even harder.

“Oh shut it,” Draco mumbles. “It’s almost time to go.”

Harry groans, “Great, and I still have homework to do.”

“Which reminds me. How are classes for you?”

“They’re okay… still struggling with potions though.”

“Seriously? Even after all these years you still don’t get it?”

“Hey! I’m trying okay! I’m better at Defense of the Dark Arts!”

“Well if that’s the case, I’ll tutor you.”

Harry looks at Draco with surprise.

“Wait really?”

“Now don’t act all surprised. That’s what friends do, is it not?”

“Good point… Thanks Draco,” Harry smiles.

Draco blushes once more as he mumbles a ‘you’re welcome.’ The two finally get up and start walking back to Hogwarts.

Harry ends up in the common room with Ron and Hermione, the three of them taking up their favorite space to finish up homework. As per usual, Hermione is helping the two boys as they struggle with their subjects. After reading about werewolves for the seventeenth time, Ron decides it would be better to take a break.

“You haven’t even written anything yet!” Hermione points out. “How can you take a break right now?”

“Come on ‘Mione, we’ve been at it for an hour now! My head hurts and I think that’s a sign for me to take a break.”

As the two are bickering once more, Harry is thinking of his own problems. One that involves a certain white blonde Slytherin.

“Harry? HARRY!” Hermione shouts.

Harry shakes his head and looks over, seeing Ron and Hermione looking at him funny.

“I asked you a question Harry. Are you alright?”

“Oh me? Yeah, yeah, I’m fine.”

Ron’s eyes narrows, “You aren’t thinking of Malfoy, are you?”

“Wha- what? No!”

“Merlin ‘Mione, he IS thinking of Malfoy!”

“Well they have been hanging out every week…”

“It’s what we agreed upon” Harry murmurs.

“Be honest with us Harry,” Ron starts. “Do you like Malfoy?”

“What?! No! No, it’s not like that,” Harry stutters out. “No, he just wants to make amends.”

“If anything he should be making amends with me,” Ron states. “He’s been insulting my family for years and all the sudden he gets a past because you’re the Chosen One.”

“He doesn’t get a pass,” Harry says. “And he will! He’s been talking about making amends with the two of you.”

“Really?” Hermione asks. “That’s surprising coming from him.”

“If that is coming from him,” Ron mumbles.

“Yes it is!” Harry shots up. “I’m going to bed. It’s getting late and we should get some sleep.”

He gathers his stuff and runs up the boys dormitory, ignoring Hermione’s protests. Harry knows deep down he’s overreacting a little, but he hates how Draco isn’t given any chances.

Fortunately for Harry and Draco, Draco is given an opportunity to amend his relationship with at least Ron. During Potions it’s decided that Ron and Draco would work together during class.

At first it’s awkward, Ron trying to figure out the book as Draco finds the ingredients. But then Harry notices Draco has some tricks up his sleeve.

“Hey Ron,” Draco says. “We need to talk.”

Ron is startled by his name being said by Draco, but he nods, ready to listen.

“I’m sorry for insulting your family all these years. I know that doesn’t make up the fact that my father hated yours and how your brother died-“

Ron winces a little at the mention of Fred.

“I most likely have a long way to go, but I want to also make it up to you.”

Draco rummages through his bag and takes out a box of chocolate frog.

“I know it isn’t much, but if I remember correctly you like chocolate frogs.”

Ron nods and carefully takes the box, observing if it’s poisoned or not.

“Trust me, it’s not poisoned.” Draco answers. “I can eat a piece if you want.”

Ron hums in response and puts the box in his bag. He then asks for Draco’s opinion on the potion and Draco explains. Once they finish their work, the two then talk about quidditch, which really opens Ron up.

During class, the two couldn’t stop talking about the World Cup.

“It’s unbelievable how Ireland’s chaser is still playing after that injury,” Draco says. “He ended up having a concussion you know.”

“Yes but he has to get better if Ireland still has the chance to be in the World Cup!” Ron points out.

At the end of class, the two came into a silent agreement of becoming less enemies and more friends. This not only surprises Harry but also Hermione, who couldn’t help but wonder how it happened.

Ron just shrugs, “He has changed for the better. I did tell him to talk to you also ‘Mione. Told him that he better make amends with my girlfriend too.”

Hermione smiles by Ron’s comment as the three walk towards the Grand Hall, ready to eat. As they walk down, Harry hears something from down the hall. He tells Hermione and Ron he’ll catch up as he quickly walks over, hearing the voices louder and louder.

“They’re just letting anyone in this school now, aren’t they?” Zacharias Smith says. “I can’t believe they even considered letting the likes of you Death Eaters in.”

Harry’s pulse quickens as he carefully looks over the scene. Zacharias and two other boys are hovering over Draco sitting down with his back against the wall. His lip is bleed as he’s sporting a black eye in the mix.

Harry knows not to step in, but deep down he is ready to jump around the corner and hex the three. But he also knows Draco can handle things on his own. At least, he thought he could.

“It’s not like that Zacharias,” Draco rasps out. “I’ve changed. I said I was sorry.”

That only angers Zacharias more as pulls Draco up by the collar and slams him into the wall. Draco makes a choking noise but makes no move to defend himself. It’s almost like he’s prepared for it and it breaks Harry’s heart.

“If you’re really sorry then do us all a favor and kill your mom and yourself.”

“That isn’t a nice thing to say Zacharias,” Harry steps in. “I thought you were better than that.”

Everyone looks startled as the four dart their eyes over to Harry, looking menacing as ever. Harry just notices the two 2nd years who clearly look like Zacharias minions.

“You have three seconds to let go of Malfoy.”

“Or what? You’re going to call the Ministry on me Chosen One?” Zacharias mocks. “You of all people should know that Draco deserves this.”

“No he doesn’t,” Harry stands firm. “You are no better than him by doing this.”

Zacharias sputters as he drops Draco and lets him slide to the ground, taking deep breaths. He marches up to Harry and whips out his wand, ready to fight. Unfortunately for him, after years of fighting experience, Harry immediately puts Zacharias and the other two boys in a Body Bind.

Draco’s eyes dart from the three then Harry, shocked of what happened. Harry just pulls him up and drags him away, leaving the three to whoever will find them.

He leads Draco to the Gryffindor tower then the boys dormitory. Draco tries protesting, telling Harry that he’s okay, but it was completely ignored. Harry pushes Draco down on his bed as he rummages through his stuff, pulling out a first aid kit.

“This is gonna hurt,” he whispers, dabbling him with some cream. Draco winces, as he tries to pull back from the pain. But Harry’s hand was firmly on the nape of his neck, leaving them in close proximity.

“Sorry, I know it hurts, but this will help. Also, before you ask, I rather not have to answer questions from Madame Pomfrey on why Zacharias and his minions are frozen.”

Draco chuckles, “For a spilt second I could see a bit of me, Crabbe, and Goyle.”

“Well, unlike them you have changed,” Harry says firmly, dabbling Draco’s lip a little more. He then looks at Draco straight into his eyes.

Both boys’ heart beats start racing more and more as they lean in closer.

“Merlin Harry!” Ron shouts. “If you’re going to snog Draco, at least do it somewhere else!”

Harry and Draco jump back and whip their heads over to Ron. He’s covering his eyes, trying to conceal the events going on in front of him.

“We weren’t snogging!” Harry sputters out. “We were just- well, we were-“

“Harry was just helping me with a few injuries,” Draco stands up. “Thank you for your help, but I should probably get going. Ron. I’ll see you both later.”

He quickly walks away, leaving the boys in the dust, not waiting for what Harry would say next. Ron looks over at him with a confused look as Harry sighs and flops back on his bed.

“Blimey,” Ron walks over and sits next to him. “You like him don’t you.”

It wasn’t a question, but a statement, since Ron could see right through him. Ever since the Battle, Ron and Hermione could read Harry like a book, just like how Harry can do the same with them.

“I do… But I don’t know what to do!”

“That’s rich coming from you.” Ron rolls his eyes. “You’re the CHOSEN ONE and yet you’re afraid of rejection?”

“Yes!” Harry groans. “Alright, yes, and I know I’m the Chosen One and the Boy Who Lived, but even those titles don’t mean anything to Draco!”

“Isn’t that a good thing? He just sees you for you then.”

Harry sits up and looks at Ron, who’s giving him a knowing look.

“Look, you and I both know he was a twat before. But even now I see he has changed. And he definitely has eyes for you mate.”

“But- I still feel bad for Ginny-“

“Don’t be. Trust me. I talked to her, and she has eyes for another person.”

“Really? Who?”

Ron smirks, “Oh wouldn’t you like to know.”

“Oh come off it Ron,” Harry chuckles, shoving him. “You know I wouldn’t get back with her again.”

“Oh alright,” Ron pauses and looks around, making sure no one else is in the room. He then whispers, “She’s dating Luna now.”

“Luna? Luna Lovegood?”

Ron nods as Harry smiles, “Good for them. I knew the two would be a good pair.”

“Really? I thought she lost her mind a little, but honestly Luna’s warming up to me.”

“Well tell Ginny I’m happy for her.”

“Good, then you have no reason to not ask Draco out.”

“Wha- what?!”

“Come on Harry, what’s really stopping you now?” Ron sighs. “Don’t make me go to Hermione.”

Harry’s face turn into a light pink as he looks down on the floor. He thinks about it for a moment, not wanting to answer Ron right away.

He sighs, “Fine. I’ll ask him out.”

Ron smiles, “Good…”

The two sit in silence once again as Ron starts to laugh a little. Harry looks at him with confusion.

“What?”

“I never thought there would be a day where I encourage my best mate to go for Draco Malfoy of all people. We’re losing it, aren’t we?”

Harry laughs and shakes his head, “I guess we are Ron.”

The following day, Draco walks up towards the Gryffindor table where Hermione, Ron, and Harry are.

“Hey Hermione. Can we talk?” Draco asks. The three freeze up in their seats, unsure what to say. Although Draco did make up with Ron and Harry, he still hasn’t made amends with Hermione.

It wasn’t like Draco wasn’t trying though. But every chance he could get, Hermione avoided him like the plague. It wasn’t until he got to Ron where Ron persuaded Hermione to get Draco a bit of a chance.

“Alone please?” he adds.

Hermione looks at both Harry and Ron as they both give her small gestures of encouragement.

She says, “Um, okay. After lunch?”

He nods, “Meet me at the courtyard. And bring your homework. We can work and talk.”

Draco walks away, leaving the three baffled. Even though Ron and Harry saw this coming they still didn’t expect Draco to do it so soon.

“He’s not wasting any time, is he?” Ron whispers as Draco walks away. “He talked to me last week…”

“Maybe he wants to start faster so he has more time to get to know us,” Harry suggests. “But I know this is hard for you both.”

“Well… if you two are giving him a chance, then I will,” Hermione confirms. “But that doesn’t erase what him and his family did to me or anyone else for the matter.”

After lunch Hermione walks over to the courtyard with Draco already sitting against a tree. He brightens up seeing Hermione walk over and set her stuff down.

“I’m glad you came,” he smiles. “I have something for you actually.”

He rummages through his bag and pulls out a cream. Hermione gives him a curious look as he explains.

“It’s a cream to get rid of your scars. I know it doesn’t erase the fact of what I said these years and what happened to you… but I at least want to give you this.”

Hermione is taken aback by his thoughtfulness and care. She almost had a hard time trying to find the right words to say.

“Thank you Draco… although this still doesn’t entirely erase what you have said to me and what your family has done…”

“Rightfully so.”

“But… it is a start,” she smiles gently. “And I appreciate it.”

The two end up quietly working on their homework, until Draco asks Hermione for help with one of his classes. The two ended up exchanging notes and talked about their favorite book genres.

Hermione was thrown with the fact that Draco loves murder mystery, but she loves the suggestions he gives. By the end of their hang out, she meets up with Ron and Harry in the Gryffindor common room.

“If I’m being quite honest, that went better than I thought,” Hermione admits. “Draco has a long way to go but… I can tell he’s trying.”

“That’s good,” Harry lets out a breath of relief. “I knew he would start making amends.”

“Yes well, if you’re seeking approval on asking him out as your boyfriend, I say go for it,” Hermione says. “Besides, you two would be good for each other.”

Harry starts blushing red as Ron starts laughing.

“Oh fuck off Ron,” Harry murmurs. “I’ll ask him… soon…”

“Right, sure you will,” Ron snickers. “He can easily defeat Voldemort yet he can’t even ask his crush out on a date.”

Hermione start laughing as Harry turns into a bright cherry red. He tells Ron to shove off and laughs with the two. Deep down he knew Hermione and Ron would come around, but he’s still happy with their reactions.

I’ll ask him out soon…

Draco is walking down the hallways, on his way to study at the library. He can feel the murmurs and whispers amongst his fellow classmates.

He’s used to this feeling of rumors flying around about him acting out as a Death Eater. Every day he wishes he could scratch off the horrid thing on his wrist. He tried every night, but it wouldn’t leave even if he tried. And trust him when he says he has tried.

“Draco!”

Draco looks up and smiles softly at the brunette boy in front of him. Harry Potter has been the only one who has been by his side after his apology.

If you would have asked his past self that he would be friends let alone have a crush on Harry Potter, he would’ve laughed at your face. But after seeing him stand up against the Dark Lord and defeat him. Draco knew he meant business.

“Hey Harry,” he meets him in the middle. “Something wrong?”

“Ah, no,” Harry chuckles. “I was just wondering if you’re doing anything today.”

“Just going to the library to study.”

“Let me join you. Ah- well, if you don’t mind that is, I mean- I understand if you don’t want the company-“

“Of course I mind,” Draco interrupts. “I mean- of course I don’t mind that you… well… join me?”

The two both look at each other for a moment then chuckle, Harry gesturing Draco to led the way. The two enter the library side by side as Draco leads them to a secluded spot.

Surrounded by shelves of books there’s only a few tables with two chairs each. One with a couple of love seats while the other has wooden chairs seen at any of the other library tables.

“I didn’t even know this area existed,” Harry realizes. “How did you find this place?”

“One day I was wandering through the library trying to find somewhere to get away from it all,” Draco answers. “Fortunately for me I found this.”

As they sit down and get their books out, Harry notices something.

“What happened to your wrist?”

Draco quickly looks down and tugs his right sleeve, attempting to cover it up.

“It’s nothing, honestly…”

Harry grabs his right arm and slow rolls up his sleeve, revealing his bandages. From Draco’s wrist down to his elbow is covered with blood seeping through.

“It- it’s fine Harry-“

“How long?”

Draco turns his head away, unwilling to look at Harry’s eyes. He feels Harry’s fingers touch his chin and move gently towards his green eyes.

With a soft command he whispers, “Draco please… I need to know…”

Draco chokes up, unsure what to tell Harry. He wants to tell him everything while also telling him nothing at once. But before he knew it, Draco is spilling his guts out.

“I just- I made so many mistakes. And this- this symbol, this mark… it reminds me of who I was. Who I don’t want to be. Every time I look at it Harry, I feel like that part of me is coming back. It haunts me in my dreams.”

Draco can feel his eyes tearing up, trying to hold back a sob. Harry pulls him into a firm hug, giving Draco an opportunity to cry. In all his years he has never been able to cry before. Not even to his own mother.

All those nights alone where he has to cover up who is destined to become. Those days where he has to pull a brave face. The nightmares he has endured when he realizes he has to murder Dumbledore, someone who he wishes he respected more.

“Draco… I’m sorry about the things you have gone through.”

Draco scoffs, “You of all people shouldn’t be. I deserve to die-“

“No you don’t,” Harry insists. “You deserve to live Draco. You deserve to have choices in your life and live the way you want.”

Draco is looking on the ground once more as Harry leans over to try to make eye contact.

“I’m proud of you Draco.”

Draco whips over with bewilderment, wondering if Harry has lost his mind. All he sees is determination and honesty. Harry sees softness within his silver eyes and tiredness from the world around him. All Harry wants to do now is wrap Draco up in a blanket and kiss him goodnight.

“Did I mention you have beautiful eyes?”

Draco chuckles, “Why no Harry, you have not…”

“Well you do,” Harry leans in closer. “And I’m enchanted by it.”

“Harry Potter are you flirting with me?”

“Maybe a little…”

“You’re such a nitwit.”

Harry pouts, “Well if that’s the case then I won’t kiss you-“

Harry’s hands start moving towards below, causing Draco to quietly moan. Harry pulls away from his lips as he kisses and bites on Draco’s neck. Draco’s hands are pulling on Harry’s hair as he leans back, giving more room for Harry to work with.

Harry licks his lips with contempt, seeing Draco’s red puffy lips pant and his long messy hair. He tucks a loose strand behind his ear, then leans in to whisper.

“Be my boyfriend.”

Draco pulls back, once again bewildered with what he has heard.

“Wha- what?”

“Obviously we should go on a date first but, my offer still stands… if you want it that is…”

Draco takes a long and good look at him, still wondering if Harry was lying or not. After a moment or two, Draco smiles and chuckles a little, shaking his head with disbelief.

“Oh alright Potter… let’s go on a date.”
