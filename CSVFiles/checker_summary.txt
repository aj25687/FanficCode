LEGEND
======================================================================
   ok  = within 3 pp  (representative)
    ~  = 3 to 5 pp    (acceptable, monitor)
    !  = 5 to 10 pp   (notable deviation)
   !!! = >10 pp       (sample may not be representative)

HOW IT WORKS:
  For multi tag fields (warnings, categories, characters,
  relationships), percentages are calculated ONLY among tags
  that matched the known top N list. This makes the comparison
  apples to apples with the AO3 population numbers.

  The 'coverage' line shows what fraction of your sample's tags
  fell within the top N. Low coverage is fine; it just means
  your sample has lots of less common tags (expected).

  For ratings, each work has exactly one, so the denominator
  is simply the number of works.
